# How 15th.plus-ex.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/15th.plus-ex.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

15th.plus-ex.com is an **anniversary type microsite** that behaves more like a poster sequence than a product UI. The system is sparse, typographic, and ceremonial: giant black display forms on light fields, minimal chrome, and composition driven by letter shapes rather than cards or dashboards.

What should survive adaptation:
- **Display type as the primary image**
- Near-empty canvases with deliberate negative space
- Black ink on pale paper-like grounds
- Navigation that stays hairline-thin so the specimen stays center stage

## Foundations

### Color

No strong multi-stop brand palette was measured. Visually the system is:
- Paper / canvas: light gray to off-white
- Ink: pure or near-black type and UI marks
- Occasional soft gray rules and panel fills

Treat color as **ink and paper**, not a multi-hue product theme.

### Typography

Declared families were not captured. Observed scale is extreme and intentional:
- Monumental display around **200–400px**, weight 700, with compressed line-height
- Secondary display ~**33px**, weight 500
- Supporting UI ~**14–20px**, weights 400–500
- Small labels ~**10–16px**

The hierarchy is almost binary: one enormous letterform or wordmark, then quiet interface type.

### Spacing and layout

- Large open margins; content floats rather than filling a dense grid
- Small radii on UI chips (~**4px** and **12px**) where controls appear
- Rhythm is poster-like: one focal mass, then a thin instrument panel

## Visual language

- Specimen-first compositions (single glyph or word at heroic scale)
- Thin top navigation, search, and cart as faint utility
- Soft panel borders rather than heavy shadows
- Grid of glyphs when browsing a full character set, still monochrome

## Components and states

- Utility header (links + account/cart)
- Hero specimen stage
- Optional side metrics panel (metrics like ascender/cap height when in exposure-like views)
- Sparse footer or none in the captured frames

States are mostly static poster frames; do not invent complex interactive chrome.

## Responsive behavior

Multiple home captures at varying crops show the same poster logic. Exact mobile breakpoints were not established — keep the specimen dominant and collapse utilities before shrinking the display face into illegibility.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** black-on-light, enormous display, empty air, thin utilities.  
**Avoid:** colorful SaaS cards, dense marketing sections, or turning the anniversary page into a standard landing-page stack.

## Scope note

Eight captures, all on `/` (“15th Anniversary”). Structured color roles were empty; guidance leans on visual reading of the frames plus measured type sizes and radii.
