# How exa.ai is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/exa.ai-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Exa is a polished developer/AI search brand. The home and customer stories sit on clean white (or near-white) canvases with black type, a strong electric blue accent (`#1f40ed`), and soft rounded cards. It feels modern API-platform marketing: confident, minimal, slightly editorial, never neon-cyber.

What should survive adaptation:
- White/light canvas with black primary type
- Blue `#1f40ed` as the single vivid accent for links/CTAs/key UI
- Radii in the 6–12px band (cards ~12, controls ~6)
- Wide section padding (~60–112px) and ~24px horizontal page padding

## Foundations

### Color

Measured mix across captures:
- `#ffffff` / `#fdfdfd` canvas
- `#000000` primary text
- `#737373` secondary
- `#1f40ed` accent blue
- Dark neutrals `#111827`, `#1f2937`, `#5f5a54` on inverted or chart contexts

Keep blue rare and purposeful; gray handles secondary hierarchy.

### Typography

Families unsupported. Observed:
- Body/UI ~14–18px / ~24–28px line-height, weight 400
- Mid titles ~28/33.6
- Display larger on heroes (visually bold marketing scale)

Taxonomy marks display/body roles, sans, card-heavy layouts.

### Spacing and layout

- Page padding 24px sides
- Large vertical section padding 60–112px
- Centered shells with ~212px side margins on wide frames
- Card grids for customers, features, blog index

Radii: 6px very common, 12px cards, 2–4–8px accents.

## Visual language

Flat surfaces, soft cards, light borders, restrained shadow. Blog and customer pages reuse the same chassis. Blue appears in links, buttons, and occasional diagram accents.

## Components and states

- Top nav + primary CTA
- Hero with short claim + dual actions
- Logo walls / customer case modules
- Blog index cards
- Content article shells

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** white + black + `#1f40ed`, soft 6–12px cards, airy sections.  
**Avoid** multi-accent rainbows and dense dashboard chrome on marketing pages.  
**Adaptation** for AI infra, search APIs, and B2B developer tools.

## Scope note

Eight captures across home, customer case, blog index, and blog post. Colors measured; font families unsupported.
