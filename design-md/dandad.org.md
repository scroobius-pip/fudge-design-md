# How dandad.org is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/dandad.org-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

D&AD is a **design-award institution on the web**: authoritative, editorial, and archive-forward. Captures cover the home, search, and New Blood archive—surfaces that prioritize **work discovery and cultural gravitas** over startup marketing chrome. Expect strong typographic hierarchy, generous indexing patterns, and a gallery/archive mindset where the **creative work is the color**.

What should survive adaptation:

- **Archive and search as primary verbs**, not feature grids.
- Calm institutional chrome that defers to case imagery.
- Clear index pages (New Blood archive) with scannable entries.
- Professional, high-trust tone—no playful SaaS mascots.
- Typography-led sectioning with restrained decoration.

## Foundations

### Color

Structured color roles were not retained on these captures. Visually treat the system as a **light institutional canvas** where photography and award work supply chroma; keep UI neutrals quiet so archives stay legible.

### Typography

Declared families not retained. Summary styles show a conventional editorial range (body around mid-teens px, larger heads for page titles). Build hierarchy with weight and size rather than ornamental display faces unless artwork supplies them.

### Spacing and layout

- Home and archive views use open marketing/archive sectioning rather than dense app sidebars.
- Search is a dedicated destination (`/search`), reinforcing findability as a first-class layout.
- New Blood archive (`/work/new-blood-archive`) behaves as a long index—rhythm matters more than card chrome.

## Visual language

- Prefer flat, content-forward surfaces; let entry imagery carry flair.
- Borders/rules may separate index rows; avoid heavy consumer-app shadows.
- Motion not established from stills.

## Components and states

### Home
Institutional landing that routes into programmes, awards, and archives.

### Search
Dedicated query surface for works/content—keep uncluttered.

### Archive index
Scannable list/grid of works with title/meta; imagery optional but valuable when present.

## Responsive behavior

Multiple desktop-oriented captures; exact mobile nav not fully specified. Keep archive scannability when narrowing—collapse multi-column indexes before shrinking type into unreadability.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**

1. Archive/search-first IA.
2. Quiet chrome, loud work.
3. Editorial title hierarchy.
4. Institutional trust over growth-hacking UI.

**Avoid**

1. Startup gradient heroes unrelated to award craft.
2. Over-cardifying every archive row into identical SaaS tiles.
3. Inventing brand accent hexes or font licences not in the data.

**Adaptation recipe**

- Neutral shell → strong index typography → work imagery → serious search.

## Scope note

Five captures across `/`, `/search`, and `/work/new-blood-archive`. Supported: page roles and archive/search character. Gaps: measured palette/type roles were sparse; font family, motion, and full responsive tokens not established.
