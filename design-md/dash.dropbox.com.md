# How dash.dropbox.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/dash.dropbox.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Dropbox Dash presents as a **product dashboard/marketing-hybrid under dash.dropbox.com**, with a general home surface and a branded collaboration moment (`/mclarenf1`). The system should feel like **Dropbox-adjacent product UI**: clean, modern, content-and-file aware, with room for partner/campaign skins without losing the base console clarity.

What should survive adaptation:

- Dashboard home as the primary orientation surface.
- Ability to host **campaign or partner experiences** (McLaren F1) inside the same product domain.
- Clean, contemporary SaaS density—not a raw enterprise admin relic.
- Clear content hierarchy for tools and featured modules.
- Flexible theming hooks for special collaborations while keeping base IA stable.

## Foundations

### Color

Structured role colors were not retained in aggregate. Visually anchor to a clean light product UI and allow campaign pages to introduce partner color in contained hero/feature regions rather than recoloring the entire shell.

### Typography

Families not declared. Product sans for UI; campaign pages may scale type up for feature storytelling.

### Spacing and layout

- Home dashboard modules in a structured product grid.
- Campaign route can open more marketing-like spacing while remaining in-product.
- Eight captures imply breadth across home states/widths plus the McLaren experience.

## Visual language

- Modern product surfaces; campaign imagery may be richer.
- Keep base dash chrome calmer than the partner skin.
- Motion not established.

## Components and states

### Dash home
Module cards, navigation, product entry points.

### Partner/campaign page (`/mclarenf1`)
Featured collaboration layout—hero media, partner marks, still clearly inside Dash.

## Responsive behavior

Multiple captures support multi-width product layout. Campaign pages should reflow media first; dash modules stack second.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**

1. Stable dash shell + swappable campaign canvas.
2. Clean product density on home.
3. Partner color contained to campaign surfaces.
4. Clear nav back into core dash.

**Avoid**

1. Letting a single partnership permanently rebrand all chrome.
2. Over-decorating utility modules.
3. Unsupported palette/font claims.

**Adaptation recipe**

- Calm product home → modular widgets → optional campaign route with richer media.

## Scope note

Eight captures on `/` and `/mclarenf1`. Supported: dual home/campaign structure. Gaps: measured color roles, fonts, deep settings screens, motion.
