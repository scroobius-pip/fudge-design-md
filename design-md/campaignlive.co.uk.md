# How campaignlive.co.uk is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/campaignlive.co.uk-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Campaign Live is a **B2B advertising trade publication**: red brand accents (`#ff0000` / `#c5001d`), charcoal type (`#1c1c1c`), dense information hierarchy, and clear membership/paywall surfaces. It feels industry-press rather than startup-SaaS—editorial authority first.

What should survive adaptation:
- Red as the only loud brand color
- Dark neutral type on light reading grounds
- Membership cards and article layouts with strong headline weight
- Professional density without chaotic clutter

## Foundations

### Color
Measured: `#ff0000`, `#c5001d` accents; text `#1c1c1c`, `#000000`, `#3c3c3c`.
Visually white/light gray page, red CTAs and logos, black headlines, muted meta gray.

### Typography
Weights span **300–900**. Sizes ~15–24px body/UI, display/headlines **32–58px**, heavy weights on titles (700–900). Feels newsy: strong heads, calmer decks.

### Spacing and layout
Tight-to-medium spacing (2–24px common) with article column + side rails on wide views. Membership pages center plan cards; article pages use classic header → deck → body.

## Visual language
- Mostly **square corners**; occasional ~6px and pill (50px) on chips/buttons
- Border-separated surfaces common
- Light mode; photography in article heroes
- Minimal soft shadow reliance

## Components and states
- Membership tier cards and subscribe CTAs (red)
- Article title block with byline/meta
- Nav with section labels
- Inline related content modules

## Responsive behavior
Membership captured near 1278–1711 wide; article at desktop. Cards reflow; red CTA emphasis remains.

## Practical guidance
**Preserve** red accent discipline, heavy news heads, membership clarity.
**Avoid** playful startup pastels or oversized empty luxury whitespace that undercuts trade-press density.

## Scope note
Studied `/membership` (multiple) and one long-form article. Homepage masthead system may add more masthead complexity.
