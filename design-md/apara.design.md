## Overview

Apara uses a spare portfolio language: white canvas, black type, rounded cards, and one electric-blue action color. The page feels calm rather than empty because the sections are built from strong contrasts: centered hero statements against wide blank fields, dark process panels against light utility areas, and product imagery that brings saturated color into an otherwise neutral system. The result is a studio presentation that feels careful, editorial, and slightly theatrical without becoming busy.

The visual rhythm depends on chapter changes more than on decorative separators. One section is almost all whitespace with a few floating cards. The next becomes a hard-edged dark panel with a split background and a bright illustration. The next turns into a pale three-card grid. Later sections shift into a centered showcase, then a dark pricing field with a glowing halo, then light social and footer utilities. That movement gives the page a clear sequence even when individual modules are simple.

What should stay consistent across the system is the restraint: small chrome, large type, broad gutters, rounded corners, and a hierarchy that lets the product images do much of the tone-setting.

## Colors

Apara’s interface palette is mostly monochrome, with blue and purple reserved for action and emphasis.

| token | value | role |
|---|---|---|
| `action` | `#2B4EFF` | Primary pills, key links, and the clearest interactive emphasis |
| `action-link` | `#0000EE` | Text links and lighter blue accents in utility areas |
| `accent-purple` | `#9453FF` | Secondary glow on pricing and small emphasis edges |
| `accent-violet` | `#7037CD` | Deeper accent used where the dark stage shifts toward violet |
| `ink` | `#000000` | Main headings and strong body copy on light surfaces |
| `muted-ink` | `#757575` | Supporting text on cards, strips, and footer copy |
| `subdued-ink` | `#A7A7A7` | Tiny labels, meta text, and softer utility notes |
| `canvas` | `#FFFFFF` | Main page ground and card base |
| `surface` | `#F9F9F9` | Light section background and elevated whitespace |
| `surface-quiet` | `#F6F6F6` | Soft utility rows and footer strips |
| `border` | `#EEEEEE` | Hairline separators and card framing |
| `surface-dark` | `#1E1E1E` | Dark panels, process blocks, and the pricing field |
| `surface-dark-deep` | `#0F0F0F` | The deepest card and footer-like dark tone |

The light side of the system uses white and near-white surfaces with nearly invisible borders, so the cards feel placed rather than boxed in. The dark side uses very low-key black and charcoal fields, then lets blue and purple serve as the only sharp highlights. Photography and illustration carry the more saturated hues: yellow, red, green, violet, and orange appear inside the art direction, while the UI itself stays disciplined. That separation keeps the brand from feeling loud even when the imagery is vivid.

## Typography

Apara’s hierarchy mixes three material families. Geist carries most of the page. Hero Font appears in the oversized opening headline. Test Söhne appears in smaller labels and quiet utility text. Licensing details for the named families were not supplied.

| token | family | size | weight | leading | tracking | use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `hero-display` | Hero Font | 4.6875rem | 400 | 1 | -0.053em | Centered opening statement and oversized brand line |
| `section-display` | Geist | 3.125rem | 500 | 1.06 | -0.026em | Major section headings such as service and pricing intros |
| `card-display` | Geist | 1.5625rem | 500 | 1.24 | -0.02em | Card titles, short project statements, and bold summaries |
| `body` | Geist | 0.875rem | 400 | 1.43 | 0em | Supporting copy, explanatory lines, and small descriptions |
| `body-strong` | Geist | 1rem | 500 | 1.5 | 0em | Buttons, emphasis lines, and short utility phrases |
| `label` | Test Söhne | 0.875rem | 400 | 1.14 | 0em | Small section tags, card labels, and understated metadata |
| `micro` | System | 0.75rem | 400 | 1 | 0em | Header chrome, legal notes, and tiny utility text |
| `utility` | Geist | 0.625rem | 400 | 1.2 | 0em | The smallest notes, badges, and counts |

The page relies on scale more than on type variety. The hero headline is almost poster-like: large, centered, and tight. Section headings are bold but less theatrical, using Geist to keep the page grounded after the opening. Supporting copy is kept compact and pale so it never competes with the imagery. Small labels sit above the main content as quiet orientation markers. The overall effect is sharp but not aggressive.

## Layout

The site is structured as a sequence of wide, low-density modules. The opening screen uses a centered message with floating cards placed around it, leaving a large amount of blank space below. That emptiness is part of the composition; it makes the card cluster feel deliberate rather than scattered. The cards are not a grid yet. They read like objects orbiting the headline.

Later sections switch to anchored layouts. The process area uses a two-part split: a dark text panel on the left and a light visual field on the right. The service section then becomes a three-column card grid on a pale ground. Each card has enough breathing room that its image and caption can read as a single unit. A pale strip below the cards acts as a secondary utility row rather than as a full section.

The selected-work area returns to a centered, almost gallery-like arrangement. A large framed product monitor occupies the middle, while smaller project tiles sit below it in a looser row. This section keeps the page from becoming too card-heavy by changing scale and alignment, not by changing the palette.

The pricing stage shifts again: a dark field with a strong radial glow, centered heading, switch-like controls, and a row of feature cards. The cards are tall, narrow, and self-contained, using glow and outline to differentiate plans. The final utility zones are shorter and more compressed. A newsletter-style row uses three colorful cards, then the footer condenses information into compact columns and a thin line of legal links. The whole page feels long, but the layout never becomes narrow or cramped because each chapter keeps generous margins and one dominant alignment choice.

## Visual language

Apara’s visual language is quiet chrome plus expressive product imagery. The cards are simple containers with rounded corners and very little decorative framing. What gives them character is the art direction inside them: a red button in a black-and-white split scene, a yellow bag on a bright stage, a monitor on a clean pedestal, and saturated product covers in the newsletter area. Those images supply the emotional temperature that the UI itself withholds.

The shapes are soft but not pillowy. Small labels and buttons use friendly radii, while larger card panels keep a more substantial corner curve. Full sections feel broader and more architectural than the cards inside them. The system does not depend on shadows; it depends on spacing, contrast, and the weight of the imagery. Borders are faint enough to disappear at a glance, which makes the white cards feel like sheets placed on paper rather than boxes stacked in a grid.

The strongest recurring motif is the blue action pill. It is small, vivid, and easy to spot against every surface. It gives the page a consistent point of tension without forcing a permanent accent bar or a heavy navigation system.

## Components

### Hero stage

- **Anatomy:** centered headline, tiny intro line, scattered floating cards, and a lot of surrounding blank space.
- **Surface:** white canvas with isolated image cards providing the only strong color.
- **Typography:** the main line uses the oversized display treatment; the smaller line uses muted Geist.
- **Shape:** cards lean on medium-rounded corners; the large title itself is visually soft because of the tight spacing.
- **Composition:** the headline stays central while the cards sit off-axis, so the page feels balanced without becoming symmetrical.
- **Visible states:** primary buttons appear as vivid blue pills; secondary text stays plain and understated.

### Process panel

- **Anatomy:** dark left text block, light right illustration field, and a hard split between the two halves.
- **Surface:** near-black body text area beside a pale stage with bright product art.
- **Typography:** the heading is large and bold; the supporting line is smaller and gray.
- **Shape:** the panel uses broad rounded corners, but the internal objects keep sharper edges.
- **Composition:** the left side explains the process; the right side dramatizes it with one large illustration and partial card reveal.
- **Visible states:** the section has no obvious decorative state change; its contrast does the work.

### Service card

- **Anatomy:** top tag, centered product image, and a short two-line promise at the bottom.
- **Surface:** white cards on a light gray field.
- **Typography:** card titles are bold and legible; labels are tiny and pale.
- **Shape:** each card is a rounded rectangle with generous internal padding.
- **Composition:** image first, statement second; the visual item anchors the card and the line below resolves the meaning.
- **Visible states:** the cards appear stable and static, with a strong resting state rather than a layered interactive one.

### Selected-work block

- **Anatomy:** section label, strong heading, large framed monitor image, and smaller project tiles below.
- **Surface:** mostly white with subtle framing around the central showcase.
- **Typography:** the heading is the main event; the labels are minimal and quiet.
- **Shape:** the main showcase is a broad rectangle with soft corners; the smaller tiles are more compact.
- **Composition:** the central image holds the eye first, then the surrounding projects provide secondary reading.
- **Visible states:** tiles read as separate items, not as dense thumbnails.

### Pricing card

- **Anatomy:** centered title, small qualifier, control-like tabs, and a row of dark pricing cards.
- **Surface:** deep black with blue and violet glow near the top and around card edges.
- **Typography:** the title is large and white; supporting copy is faint and restrained.
- **Shape:** tall cards with rounded corners and a slightly luminous outline.
- **Composition:** the cards are evenly spaced and horizontally aligned; the center card gets the strongest emphasis.
- **Visible states:** outlined emphasis and brighter glows mark the featured plan without changing the base structure.

### Newsletter strip and footer

- **Anatomy:** colorful feature tiles, a low-profile text row, then compact footer columns and legal links.
- **Surface:** white and very light gray, with the footer tightening the tone.
- **Typography:** small body copy and muted metadata dominate here.
- **Shape:** soft rounded tiles above, tighter utility containers below.
- **Composition:** the colorful cards act as a final visual lift before the page closes on a more informational register.
- **Visible states:** the action pill remains the clearest interactive object in the entire zone.

## Responsive behavior

On narrower widths, the structure should keep the same story order even if the arrangement changes. The hero should keep the headline before the floating cards, not after them. Three-up card grids should collapse in a way that preserves each card’s internal reading order: label, image, then statement. The centered showcase should keep its large visual anchor first and move the smaller tiles beneath it. The pricing area should reduce horizontal crowding before it reduces the importance of the centered title. Utility strips should wrap only after the main action remains readable. The page depends more on spacing than on dense borders, so the first responsive move should be to preserve breathing room, not to add separators.

## Practical implementation guidance

### Preserve

- Keep the opening headline oversized and spare.
- Use white or near-white surfaces as the default, then reserve dark fields for special chapters.
- Keep the blue pill as the main action signal across the page.
- Let imagery carry color; the chrome should stay calm.
- Keep card corners generous and consistent so the page reads as one system.

### Avoid

- Avoid adding a second action color for routine links.
- Avoid busy shadows or layered glows on every card.
- Avoid thin, fussy borders that fight the blank space.
- Avoid shrinking type too early; the system depends on confident scale.
- Avoid turning the utility rows into full promotional blocks.

### Recommended build order

1. Establish the core palette: canvas, ink, border, action blue, and one dark surface.
2. Build the hero stage and the section-heading scale.
3. Reuse one card shell for services, work tiles, and newsletter tiles.
4. Add the split dark process panel.
5. Add the pricing stage with the luminous dark cards.
6. Finish with the utility strip and footer.

### Accessibility

- Keep dark text on light surfaces and white text on dark surfaces at strong contrast.
- Do not rely on color alone to distinguish the action state; the blue pill should also differ in shape and fill.
- Keep small labels readable at their intended size and avoid compressing letter spacing further.
- Make image alternatives descriptive enough to explain the role of the image in the section.
- Keep visible focus styling obvious on the blue controls so keyboard users can track the page.

## Scope note

This guide covers the homepage chapters shown here: the hero, process split, service grid, selected-work block, pricing stage, newsletter strip, and footer. It does not define exact motion, hover detail, alternate mobile composition, or fine-grained interaction choreography. Type and spacing values follow the site’s 2px step.
