# How ashmolean.org is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/ashmolean.org-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

The Ashmolean site is **institutional calm**: warm light pages, near-black text (`#231f20`), gray secondary (`#444444`), and photography of objects and spaces doing the emotional work. Typography mixes approachable sans with occasional serif display for heritage campaigns. It feels like a museum brochure translated to the web—generous, legible, unflashy.

What should survive adaptation:

- Monochrome light foundation; color mostly from photography.
- Readable body ~16–17.6px with open leading (~22–25px).
- Wide content measures with large side padding systems (~64px, sometimes optical 640px full-bleed tricks).
- Minimal radii/shadows—flat, print-like sections.

## Foundations

### Color

Measured text `#231f20`, `#000000`, secondary `#444444`. Backgrounds white/warm off-white. Accents, if any, are restrained and secondary to images.

### Typography

Sans dominant at 300–600 weight; body 16–17.6/22.8–25.1; headings ~28.8/31.7; larger display ~35/50. Serif appears on some campaign headings (taxonomy notes serif on appeal).

### Spacing and layout

Section padding 16–32px common; side padding 32–64px. Full-bleed image bands break the centered text column. Longform stays single-column for readability.

## Visual language

- Flat surfaces; rare 1px borders.
- Photography-led heroes and inline images.
- Little corner-radius language—rectangular, editorial.

## Components and states

- Global museum header/nav.
- Hero with welcome messaging.
- Visit info cards/lists.
- Campaign story blocks and donation/appeal CTAs.
- Footer with practical visitor links.

## Responsive behavior

Includes narrower crops (~780–1046) alongside ~1712 desktop—content stacks, type stays readable. Exact breakpoint tokens not measured.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** quiet institutional monochrome, photo heritage, longform readability.

**Avoid:** startup gradients, dense card marketplaces, novelty display fonts that undercut museum authority.

## Scope note

Home, plan-your-visit, appeal, and working pages. Stronger on type/spacing than on decorative tokens.
