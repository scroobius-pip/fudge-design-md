## Overview

Artefakt.mov uses a severe black-and-white language that feels closer to a quiet film slate than to a conventional studio homepage. The page is built around one central idea: the brand mark is the main event, and everything else stays small, precise, and far enough away to avoid stealing attention. A narrow band of utility text sits along the top edge, the middle of the screen holds a dense character-built wordmark, and the bottom edge closes with short captions that read like credits. The result is controlled, spare, and deliberately unsentimental.

The composition relies on absence as much as on content. Most of the frame is empty black space, and that empty field gives the small white type a crisp, poster-like presence. The page does not use color to sort meaning, and it does not lean on boxes, pills, or visual separators. Instead, it uses placement, scale, and the density of the central mark to build hierarchy. That makes the system feel disciplined and editorial, with a slight mechanical edge.

The strongest impression is that the interface has been reduced to essentials without losing personality. The top rail, the middle logo block, and the lower captions all speak in the same compact voice, but each zone plays a different role in the frame. The brand mark anchors the identity, the utility text frames it, and the lower statements finish the composition with a calm, factual tone.

## Colors

The color system is intentionally binary. Black is the field, and white is the signal. There is no separate accent tone, no tinted surface system, and no soft background palette carrying secondary meaning. The top navigation, logo, footer lines, and the central character-built mark all sit in white against the black field, which gives the page its sharp contrast and its direct reading path.

The black surface is not merely empty; it behaves like the page itself. A faint screen-grid texture sits inside the darkness, so the field feels slightly technical and display-like rather than flat or paper-like. That texture does not become a second brand color. It stays inside the same dark family and keeps the monochrome rule intact. The page works because it never steps outside that narrow range.

There is also no separate light mode in the supplied frame. The identity lives in the black field, and the white text is the only clearly legible interface color. The central mark has an image-like density, but it still belongs to the same black-and-white system. That consistency keeps the page from drifting into decorative contrast or mood lighting. For this design, white is for content, black is for space, and nothing else is needed.

## Typography

Tronica Mono is the only material family in the supplied material. It gives the site its coded, engineered tone. The visible text is small, even, and compact, with uppercase letterforms that sit like terminal captions. The 14.3333px size paired with 15.7667px leading creates a tight but legible rhythm; the type feels exact rather than airy. That scale works because the page does not ask the type to carry long-form reading. It only needs to label, locate, and frame the central mark.

The typography is not doing the usual work of a marketing site. There is no display ladder, no broad range of headings, and no body-copy system with many size steps. Instead, the hierarchy is almost entirely positional. The logo at top left is the strongest brand treatment, while the other lines remain small and plain. The bottom captions feel like credits because they use the same compact voice as the top rail. That consistency is part of the brand character: the page speaks in one register from top to bottom.

The single family should remain the default for every text role shown here: navigation, contact details, location text, the small footer statements, and any short legal line. Mixing in another family would weaken the page’s controlled, mechanical feel. Typeface licensing is not stated in the packet; confirm use rights before shipping Tronica Mono.

## Layout

The layout is built from four anchors: the brand mark at the upper left, the compact navigation group near the top center, the contact line toward the upper right, and the studio/location block at the far right edge. Those anchors live inside a very tight horizontal inset, so the top band feels framed rather than padded. The same narrow inset appears on the lower edge, which ties the page together and keeps the composition from drifting.

The center of the page is almost entirely open. Into that open space sits the main wordmark, stretched wide across the midline and built from dense repeated characters. It reads more like a graphic object than a heading. The mass is horizontally broad but still visually light because it is made of tiny marks and gaps rather than filled shapes. That gives the page a paradoxical quality: the strongest element is also the most porous.

The vertical spacing is equally deliberate. The top rail stays high, the central mark sits well below it, and the bottom captions rest close to the lower edge. This creates a slow three-part reading order: first the site identity and utility text, then the central emblem, then the closing lines. The black field between those zones is not dead space; it is the thing that makes the rest of the layout feel composed. The page depends on that silence.

The repeated edge inset matters because it keeps the entire frame aligned to one visual rule. A future build should preserve that exact feeling: narrow margins, a large central void, and edge-anchored text that never wanders into the middle unless it belongs to the main mark.

## Visual language

Artefakt.mov turns typography into texture. The central logo is assembled from many small white glyphs, creating a block of character noise that still resolves into a single brand shape. That treatment gives the page a coded, engineered mood without adding literal interface chrome. Small stray glyphs drift around the field, which adds a sense of motion even though the page itself is still. They read like fragments, calibration marks, or signal artifacts.

The page avoids the usual decorative shortcuts. There are no visible panels, no rounded containers, no gradients, no icon sets, and no shadow language. The only visual variation comes from the density of the character field and the subtle tonal texture in the black background. That restraint makes the white text feel very exact. The brand voice is not warm or glossy; it is measured, technical, and a little austere.

The lowercase mark at the top left gives the system a recognizable anchor, but the central field is what gives it identity. That middle block should stay imperfect in a controlled way. It is most effective when it feels assembled from repeating units rather than drawn as a clean vector shape. The surrounding captions then function like labels around a machine part: short, factual, and calm.

## Components

### Header bar

- **Anatomy:** Left-aligned brand mark, small navigation group, contact line, and a right-aligned studio/location block.
- **Typography:** The same Tronica Mono voice throughout, at the compact micro size.
- **Surface:** Black field only; no separate bar, no border, no fill.
- **Composition:** The elements are spaced widely across the top edge so the center remains open.
- **Visible states:** The header is steady and minimal; it does not depend on pills, underlines, or badge treatment for impact.

### Central wordmark field

- **Anatomy:** A wide character-built logotype spanning the center of the screen, with small scattered glyphs around it.
- **Surface:** Directly on black, with the white mark acting as both text and image.
- **Typography:** Dense repeated characters create the mass; the result feels more like a constructed graphic than a line of copy.
- **Composition:** The mark sits horizontally across the middle and leaves substantial black space above and below.
- **Hierarchy:** This is the dominant element on the page. Everything else exists to frame it.

### Footer captions

- **Anatomy:** A short two-line slogan at bottom left and a longer two-line studio statement at bottom right.
- **Typography:** Same mono family, same compact scale, same all-white treatment.
- **Spacing:** The text is pinned close to the lower edge but still has enough breathing room to avoid feeling cramped.
- **Composition:** The two blocks balance each other across the width and close the page without turning into a paragraph layout.
- **Tone:** These lines should feel like credits or descriptors, not like marketing copy.

### Page surface

- **Anatomy:** Deep black background with a faint screen-grid texture.
- **Role:** The surface is part of the identity, not just a backdrop.
- **Treatment:** Keep the texture subtle enough that the field still reads as a single monochrome plane.
- **Relationship to content:** The surface makes the white text look crisp and slightly isolated, which suits the brand.

## Responsive behavior

The desktop composition depends on wide negative space, so responsive handling should protect that feeling rather than compress everything into a dense stack. When the viewport narrows, the first priority is to keep the top brand and utility text legible, then preserve the central wordmark as the main visual anchor, and only then decide how the bottom captions wrap. The layout should still feel like one frame, not like a grid of disassembled parts.

If the center mark must shrink, it should do so while keeping its character density intact. The page should not replace the mark with a plain text heading, because the mark is the identity. Top and bottom text can wrap to two lines if needed, but the alignment logic should remain strict: left anchors stay left, right anchors stay right, and the middle stays reserved for the brand object.

Spacing should scale conservatively. The tight edge inset is part of the style, but on smaller widths it may need a little more room to keep the compact mono text from colliding with the frame edge. Even then, the relationship between the edge text and the open center should remain obvious. The page can compress, but it should not lose the sense of a black stage with a single central object.

## Practical implementation guidance

### Preserve

- Keep the entire system monochrome. White text on black space is the brand.
- Preserve the single-family typographic voice and the compact 14.3333px / 15.7667px reading size.
- Keep the top rail split across the width and the bottom captions pinned to the edges.
- Preserve the large empty center around the main wordmark.
- Treat the central logo as a graphic field built from repeated characters, not as ordinary heading text.

### Avoid

- Avoid introducing accent colors, gradients, tinted cards, or soft surface layers.
- Avoid adding decorative borders, shadows, or rounded containers.
- Avoid mixing in another font family or building a large type scale that would make the page feel like a brochure.
- Avoid crowding the center with extra copy, badges, or callouts.
- Avoid turning the page into a generic portfolio grid; the sparse poster structure is the point.

### Recommended build order

1. Establish the black canvas and the narrow edge inset.
2. Set the Tronica Mono micro type for the utility lines and footer text.
3. Place the top-left brand mark and the right-aligned header clusters.
4. Build the central character-built wordmark as the primary visual object.
5. Add the bottom-left slogan and bottom-right statement.
6. Check the spacing relationship between the top rail, the center mark, and the footer band at the target desktop width.

### Accessibility

- Keep contrast high: white text should remain clean and readable against the black field.
- If the top-left mark or any utility label is interactive, give it a clear accessible name and a visible focus style.
- Do not rely on the tiny floating glyph fragments alone to communicate meaning; the actual site name and labels should remain readable.
- If the central wordmark is decorative, pair it with an accessible text alternative elsewhere on the page.
- Keep link and control targets large enough to use without precision pointing.

## Scope note

This guide covers the desktop home page: the top wordmark rail, the central glyph-built brand block, and the two lower caption groups. It does not include mobile collapse, scroll motion, hover or focus styling, interior pages, or additional type licensing detail beyond the short note above.
