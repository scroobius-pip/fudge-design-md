# How arcee.ai is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/arcee.ai-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Arcee AI presents as a **cinematic dark brand landing**: near-black full-bleed atmosphere, a restrained sans wordmark, and almost no UI chrome. The site sells intelligence through void, glow, and typography rather than dashboards or illustrations clutter.

What should survive adaptation:

- Dark immersive canvas with subtle atmospheric texture/glow.
- Sparse top navigation and generous empty field around the logo/hero.
- Limited type sizes—body ~16/24, stronger titles ~24/36, occasional large display ~48.
- 1px structural borders used carefully, not card grids.

## Foundations

### Color

No measured role hexes. Visually: black/charcoal ground, white or off-white type, possible cool blue or violet atmospheric highlights in the background plate. Keep accents rare.

### Typography

Sans, weights 400 and 700 observed. Body 16px/24px; headings 24px/36px; large display around 48px with ~57.6px line-height.

### Spacing and layout

Heavy section padding (~40px common, 80px on larger bands). Content feels centered and airy; horizontal padding ~40px.

## Visual language

- Minimal borders (1px) around some containers.
- Little to no radius language recorded.
- Mood from background art/photography, not component shadows.

## Components and states

- Top nav with few text links.
- Centered brand/hero lockup.
- Sparse supporting copy blocks.
Detailed interactive components were not strongly present in the captures.

## Responsive behavior

Two home crops at different heights; both desktop widths. No multi-breakpoint system established.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** darkness, emptiness, typographic restraint.

**Avoid:** light SaaS card grids, rainbow gradients, dense feature walls that break the cinematic quiet.

## Scope note

Only homepage captures (two viewports). Product UI and marketing interior pages not in set; color roles unsupported.
