# How dotprolabs.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/dotprolabs.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

DotPro Labs is a **dark-mode creative engineering studio** site that shouts in billboard type. The hero stacks **DESIGN / DEVELOP** in oversized cream condensed letters and slaps a fluorescent **DEPLOY** sticker in hot orange. Glassmorphic 3D orbs float in the void. Below, logo proof and case-study mockups keep the black field. It is aggressive, portfolio-forward, and unapologetically agency.

What should survive adaptation:

- Pure black canvas
- Monumental cream display type
- One violent orange accent for stamps/highlights
- Outlined pill CTAs
- Real product screenshots as proof

## Foundations

### Color

Measured palette includes:

- Oranges: `#ff4000`, `#f97316`, `#ff7e2d`
- Cream type/surfaces: `#f7efd4`, `#fafafa`, `#ececec`, `#d4d4d4`, `#aeaeae`

Text on black is cream/off-white; orange is reserved for emphasis (DEPLOY block, underline flourishes, inline highlights).

### Typography

Declared families unknown. Display is ultra-large condensed sans, cream on black. Body is smaller light gray. Nav is simple uppercase/wide tracking labels. The “OUR WORK” section repeats oversized cream headlines with a hand-orange underline squiggle.

### Spacing and layout

Radii **4 / 8 / 12 / 24**. Hero is full-viewport billboard; work section becomes a mockup grid. Marquee ticker of services can run on the top edge in orange. Stats (22+ ideas, 48+ designs) sit as large numerals in the hero right rail.

## Visual language

- 3D translucent orbs / blobs
- Sticker/stamp typography treatments
- Thin outlined pills (“BOOK A CALL”, “CONTACT US”)
- Dark portfolio screenshots with colorful product UI inside
- Occasional handwritten orange marks

## Components and states

- **Nav:** wordmark left, text links center, outline pill right
- **Hero CTAs:** outline pills, not filled orange slabs (orange is the stamp)
- **Logo wall:** muted monochrome marks on black
- **Case cards:** browser frames / product shots

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**

- Black + cream + orange stamp logic
- Billboard type scale
- Outline pills vs orange graphic accents
- Portfolio mockups on dark

**Avoid**

- Soft light SaaS restyle
- Filling every button orange (dilutes the stamp)
- Tiny polite headlines
