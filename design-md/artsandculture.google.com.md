# How artsandculture.google.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/artsandculture.google.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Google Arts & Culture is a **media-first cultural magazine** built on Google’s light system: white canvas, Google Blue links (`#1a73e8`), gray body (`#202124` / `#3c4043` / `#80868b`), and generous story cards. Art and photography always outrank chrome; UI is a quiet librarian for huge cultural media.

What should survive adaptation:

- Light surfaces and blue text/icon accents.
- Image-led cards with ~8px corners.
- Clear type hierarchy from 11–14px meta up to 36–70px display.
- Centered content with large side margins (~252px on some views).

## Foundations

### Color

Measured: accent `#1a73e8` and white-on-dark moments `#ffffff`; text `#202124`, `#000000`, `#3c4043`, `#80868b`. Palette stays limited so artwork color isn’t fighting brand color.

### Typography

Sans (Google Sans / Roboto-like visually). Body 14/20; titles 22–36/28–44; experiment display 70/70 bold. Weights 400–700; occasional thin 100 display.

### Spacing and layout

Card grids with 8–24px padding. Home stories use tall media blocks. Horizontal centering common on explore/recommended.

## Visual language

- 8px card radius is the workhorse (also 2, 16, 20 occasionally).
- Borders sparse; separation via whitespace and image edges.
- Little shadow reliance—flat modern Google.

## Components and states

- Top app bar with logo, search, profile.
- Story/editorial cards and horizontal rails.
- Category chips / filters.
- Experiment hero with massive type.
- Footer cultural/legal links.

## Responsive behavior

Multiple widths including shorter crops; card grids reflow. Detailed mobile nav not fully documented here.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** artwork supremacy, Google blue sparingly, 8px media cards, airy magazine rhythm.

**Avoid:** heavy dark skins by default; loud multi-accent chips; tiny text over busy art without scrims.

## Scope note

Home, explore, recommended, and Art Remix experiment. Structured media analysis limited; visual read from screenshots.
