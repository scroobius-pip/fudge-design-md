# How anthropic.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/anthropic.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Anthropic’s site is **editorial restraint as brand**: warm off-white paper, near-black ink, generous margins, and typography that feels closer to a thoughtfully printed essay than a SaaS launch page. Home, engineering notes, and institute pieces share a bookish calm—authority without neon futurism.

What should survive adaptation:
- Warm paper canvas (not cold pure white)
- Serif (or highly classical) display for big ideas; clean sans for UI
- Extreme whitespace and long measure control
- Almost monochrome chrome; color appears mainly in content imagery if at all

## Foundations

### Color

No stable measured palette roles were retained across these pages; rely on the visual description. The lived system is cream/off-white ground, black primary text, muted brown-gray secondary text, and minimal accent. Do not invent a bright purple “AI” primary—the brand’s power is refusal of that cliché.

Large classical headlines; comfortable reading sizes for long engineering/institute articles; small nav labels. Hierarchy is literary: title → deck → body → captions. Tracking and line-height should feel print-informed.

### Spacing and layout

Recurring spacing measurements include 16px, 32px, 48px, 64px, 96px, 152.5px, 156px, 316px. Wide margins, single focused columns for articles, sparse home modules. Sections breathe. Observed corner radii include 8px. Cards are understated when used at all.

## Visual language

Hairlines, quiet rules, few shadows. Imagery—if present—is documentary or diagrammatic, not glossy stock. Buttons are simple dark pills or text links; never shouty gradients.

## Components and states

- Minimal top nav with wordmark
- Hero essay titles
- Article layouts with clear H1 and long prose
- Inline links in restrained emphasis
- Footer with simple textual IA

## Responsive behavior

Captures include wide home and article widths (~1712–2048px). On smaller viewports keep the paper field, reduce display size, and maintain readable line length rather than adding multi-column clutter.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** paper/ink calm, classical type contrast, essay pacing.  
**Avoid** cyberpunk dark mode defaults, rainbow model badges as the visual system, cramped marketing grids.

## Scope note

Studied 8 page captures on paths /, /engineering/harness-design-long-running-apps, /institute/recursive-self-improvement. Some structural families were incomplete on these pages. Home plus engineering and institute articles were studied; Claude app UI is not included.
