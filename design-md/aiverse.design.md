## Overview

aiverse.design presents AI products as a calm editorial library rather than a glossy software pitch. The page stays on a warm paper canvas, then layers in black serif statements, soft beige cards, and rounded screenshots that do the explanatory work. The result feels collected and instructive: one part playbook, one part gallery, with enough breathing room that each example can read as a small exhibit.

The homepage leads with a compact subscription block and a collage of AI interface screenshots. The patterns area shifts into a browse-first structure with filters, counts, and a large card grid. A centered framework section follows, using a serif headline and a procession of warm phase cards to explain the system. The footer closes the page with small, quiet links. Across all of it, restraint is the style: few colors, few effects, and no decorative clutter competing with the content.

## Colors

| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| action | `#000000` | Primary buttons, headlines, core UI text |
| ink | `#000000` | Main text and strong labels |
| muted ink | `#877164` | Secondary copy, metadata, subdued labels |
| muted ink soft | `#AA9E92` | Quiet helper text and tertiary labels |
| canvas | `#FFF9F5` | Page background and open space |
| surface | `#F4EDE7` | Soft panels, chips, and inset areas |
| surface soft | `#EAD9C9` | Card fills and warm module blocks |
| surface strong | `#E5CEBA` | Deeper beige modules and framed sections |
| border | `#E2D9CF` | Hairline card borders and chip strokes |
| accent | `#EC722F` | Small stars, markers, and tiny brand details |

The palette is deliberately shallow. Cream and paper tones carry the layout, black provides all high-contrast structure, and taupe values soften the hierarchy without making the page feel gray. The orange accent stays small and lively; it belongs to star clusters, badges, and tiny decorative marks, not to large blocks or primary actions. The screenshot tiles bring their own image colors, but the interface itself stays on the cream, black, and taupe system, so the page reads as a warm frame around varied example imagery. There is no dark counterpart, so the light palette has to carry the whole system.

## Typography

Louize is the display voice. System is the interface voice. That split is the backbone of the page: Louize delivers the bookish, high-contrast statements; System keeps the library, controls, and card metadata plain and readable. The serif face appears only in the big editorial moments, which makes it feel intentional rather than ornamental.

Font licensing is not stated here.

| token | family | size | weight | leading | tracking | use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| hero-display | Louize | 3.75rem | 400 | 0.8 | -0.05em | Home hero headline |
| section-display | Louize | 6rem | 400 | 1 | -0.05em | Framework and large statement sections |
| card-title | System | 1.5rem | 700 | 1.33 | 0em | Section cards and strong module titles |
| body | System | 1rem | 400 | 1.5 | 0em | Supporting copy and descriptions |
| body-medium | System | 1rem | 500 | 1.5 | 0em | Buttons, labels, and emphasized body text |
| label | System | 0.875rem | 500 | 1.43 | 0em | Navigation, filters, and small chip text |
| eyebrow | System | 0.75rem | 600 | 1.33 | 0.05em | All-caps phase labels and small headers |
| legal-copy | System | 0.75rem | 400 | 1.33 | 0em | Footer links and fine print |

The display sizing should stay dramatic and loose. Louize needs tight tracking, short line lengths, and enough vertical space that its loops and terminals can breathe. The body text is much flatter and more practical, which keeps the page from tipping into costume. Keep the serif headlines at 60px and 96px scale points where the layout allows; keep the System text anchored to 12px, 14px, 16px, 18px, 20px, and 24px steps. The hierarchy depends more on size and contrast than on many weights.

## Layout

The home hero is a two-part composition. On the left, a pill kicker, a large statement, a short paragraph, and a single input-and-button row sit inside a broad rounded cream block. On the right, a collage of screenshot tiles climbs vertically and laterally, creating the sense of a curated wall of examples. The spacing is generous enough that the layout never feels busy, even with many thumbnails present. The rounded block shape matters as much as the text; it gives the hero a contained, card-like presence on an otherwise open canvas.

The browse view changes the rhythm. A top bar introduces the section with a count and a right-aligned action button. Under that, pills for phases and category filters sit on one line, with soft borders and quiet fills. The content below becomes a three-column grid of large cards, each with a wide media area and a short caption. The grid uses consistent gutters and conservative framing, so the screenshots remain the main event. The left rail in the patterns area adds a second layer of structure: compact section names, indented subitems, and small counts give the page a navigable spine.

The framework section centers a large serif headline and then spreads five tall cards across the width of the page. The cards have a slight angled or beveled edge, which makes them feel like paper panels rather than strict rectangles. Their fills step through near-white, sand, tan, and deeper beige, so the row reads as a progression. Beneath or beside that section, the larger explanatory panel uses a wide warm block with a curved cutout and a smaller card grid. That combination of a big editorial slab plus compact supporting cards gives the page a strong chapter break.

The footer lowers the energy without changing the language. It uses a shallow horizontal strip, a small brand seal, muted link columns, and legal text that sits far below the main content. The structure is compact and orderly, which makes the whole page feel finished rather than truncated.

## Visual language

The site’s visual language is quiet, framed, and example-led. Screenshots are the hero imagery. They appear inside rounded cards with thin borders, pale fills, and very little shadow. The cards are not trying to look like floating glass objects; they look like printed plates pinned onto a paper board. That choice keeps attention on the interfaces inside the images.

The page also relies on a few recurring shapes. Pills are used for the subscription button, the search/control chips, and small badges. Their rounding is high but not cartoonish. Larger sections use 16px card corners and broader warm panels. Some framework cards add a subtle angle or cut edge, which gives the row motion without actual animation. Small orange stars and markers break up the beige palette and give the system a mild handcrafted note.

The atmosphere is editorial and tidy, not technical and dense. There is space around everything. Even the dense browse grid still feels open because the cards are separated by clear canvas gaps and the text stays compact. The whole system depends on the relationship between softness and clarity: warm surfaces, hard black text, restrained accents, and careful alignment.

## Components

### Top navigation

The header uses a tiny round brand mark, simple text links, a bookmark icon, a search chip, a black subscription pill, and a login link. The controls sit in a single clean row and never dominate the page. The search control is especially restrained: a light capsule with a small icon and a keyboard hint, not a heavy input.

### Home hero

The hero is built as a rounded panel with a left-aligned copy stack and a right-side screenshot collage. The kicker is a small cream pill. The heading is large and black. The supporting text sits in muted taupe and stays short. The input field is soft, wide, and low-contrast, while the submit button is black and compact. Beneath that, the star row and the “loved by” line give the block a social proof note without adding visual weight. The collage should stay varied, with multiple screenshot frames rather than one large image.

### Search and filter strip

The browse controls use pill chips with light fills, thin borders, and modest labels. They are practical first and decorative second. The group should stay horizontal and readable, with enough spacing that each control looks tappable. The count text and the right-aligned action button create a useful tension: one side says where you are, the other side offers a way deeper in.

### Pattern cards

The gallery cards are the main library unit. Each card uses a pale canvas, a hairline border, rounded corners, and a large media area at the top. Many include a small black NEW badge. Captions sit below the image in straightforward black text. The cards need to feel like examples, not product ads, so the framing stays modest and the typography stays quiet. The media can vary in color, but the card shell should stay consistent.

### Phase cards and framework blocks

The phase cards are the most distinctive structural element. Each one is tall, softly beveled, and filled with a warm neutral from the same family as the canvas. The label is small and uppercase. The title is bold. The question text is ordinary and explanatory. A small footer line or count sits low in the card. The cards work best when arranged as a row with even spacing so the progression reads clearly.

The larger framework block uses a serif headline and a broad warm panel with a curved edge or inset shape. It should feel like a chapter opener. Supporting cards below it can be more compact and more system-like, but the big panel should stay calm and spacious.

### Footer

The footer is spare and low contrast. It uses the smallest text on the page, grouped into neat columns. Links should remain legible and tidy, and the brand mark should stay small. This section should close the page, not restart it.

## Responsive behavior

On narrower screens, the hero should stack into copy first, then the control row, then the screenshot collage. The collage can become a shorter scrollable or stacked block, but it should not be squeezed into illegibility. The browse filters should stay on one line if possible; if not, they should scroll horizontally rather than wrap into a tall column. The three-column gallery should reduce to two columns and then one, while keeping card radius, border, and caption spacing intact.

The framework cards should collapse into a vertical sequence, but the warm progression of fills should remain visible. The large serif headlines should step down carefully: keep them oversized, but move from 6rem-scale statements to 3.75rem or 3rem when width demands it. The footer can stack into fewer columns while preserving the light, quiet finish. Across every breakpoint, keep the page airy and avoid dense compression.

## Practical implementation guidance

### Preserve

Keep the warm canvas, the black serif hero voice, the System UI hierarchy, and the card-and-chip rounding. Keep action colors black. Keep the orange accent tiny. Keep borders thin and beige, not gray. Keep screenshots framed as examples, not as floating art objects.

### Avoid

Avoid bright blue or purple AI branding. Avoid glass effects, heavy shadows, and chrome that fights the page background. Avoid mixing extra fonts into the body system. Avoid overfilling the layout with dense callouts, aggressive labels, or dark panels. Avoid turning every module into a separate style; the page works because repeated components share the same soft shell.

### Recommended build order

Start with the color and type tokens. Then build the top nav and the black primary action. Add the hero block next, including the input row and the screenshot collage. After that, build the browse controls and gallery cards. Finish with the framework cards, the large explanatory panel, and the footer. This order mirrors the visual hierarchy of the page and keeps the shared card language consistent.

### Accessibility

Keep black text on cream and beige surfaces at strong contrast. Make the pill controls and links visibly focusable. Keep the card captions useful on their own, since the screenshots carry meaning but do not replace text. Use the orange accent only as support, not as the sole cue for state. Ensure the search chip, filter chips, subscription button, and gallery links can be reached in a sensible reading order.

## Scope note

This guide covers the home hero, browse grid, framework phases, and footer on the cream aiverse landing page. Mobile collapse rules, motion, hover states, and the blank listed frame are not defined here.
