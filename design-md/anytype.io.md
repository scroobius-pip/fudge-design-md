# How anytype.io is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/anytype.io-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Anytype markets itself as a **dark, local-first knowledge universe**: black canvas, soft gradient orbs, large white sans headlines, and product screenshots floating in rounded frames. Pricing continues the dark system with comparison cards and a clear highlighted plan. It feels cosmic and calm rather than corporate light-mode productivity.

What should survive adaptation:
- True black/near-black marketing canvas
- Soft atmospheric gradients without rainbow noise
- Large white headlines + muted gray body
- Rounded product frames and pricing cards with subtle borders

## Foundations

### Color

No stable measured palette roles were retained across these pages; rely on the visual description. Visual anchors: black ground, white type, gray secondary, quiet accent for selected pricing tier or CTAs (often a soft warm or cool highlight—keep it singular). Screenshots may introduce internal app colors; the shell stays dark.

### Spacing and layout

Recurring spacing measurements include 24px, 32px, 36px, 40px, 62px, 64px, 80px, 96px, 106px, 128px, 136px. Full-width dark bands, hero with orb atmosphere, feature sections alternating copy/screenshot, pricing multi-column cards. Observed corner radii include 6px, 14px, 20px, 44px.

## Visual language

Glow orbs, soft vignettes, thin card borders on dark, gentle shadows under screenshots. Icons are simple. Avoid cluttering space—emptiness is part of the cosmos metaphor.

## Components and states

- Dark header with wordmark + Pricing + CTAs
- Hero headline + subcopy + primary download/get CTA
- Feature rows with product UI frames
- Pricing comparison cards; emphasized recommended plan
- FAQ or footer textual blocks

## Responsive behavior

Home and pricing captures (~1432–1504px) are desktop. On smaller screens stack pricing cards, keep orbs subtle so they do not overpower type, and collapse nav.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** dark cosmic shell, screenshot frames, pricing card clarity, muted secondary text.  
**Avoid** forcing a white Notion-like shell that erases the brand night sky.

## Scope note

Studied 8 page captures on paths /, /pricing. Some structural families were incomplete on these pages. Home and pricing were primary; in-app Anytype desktop UI beyond marketing screenshots is not fully specified.
