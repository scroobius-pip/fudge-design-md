# How browserbase.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/browserbase.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Browserbase sells browser infrastructure for AI agents with a **sharp, technical** visual voice: high contrast surfaces, very large display lines (~56–66px), compact UI labels (14–20px), **2px borders**, and tiny radii (1–2px) that feel closer to terminals and schematics than soft consumer SaaS. Home and pricing share the same austere precision.

What should survive adaptation:

- **High contrast, low ornament**
- **Huge technical display** with short supporting copy
- **Hairline/2px structure** and near-square corners
- **Pricing as system diagrams** more than friendly cards
- **Developer seriousness** without cartoon illustration

## Foundations

### Color

Structured role colors were empty; screenshots read as black/white/off-black with a single accent used sparingly for links or key CTAs. Prefer monochrome infrastructure palette; add one accent only if needed for primary actions.

### Typography

- Display: **56–66px** regular (also 40–58px band)
- UI/body: **14–16px** and **20px** (weights 400/500)
- Some ultra-small **1px** artifacts are noise—not a real style
- Line-heights often tight or default; display feels poster-like

### Spacing and layout

- Horizontal padding **32–44px**
- Large vertical section padding (~**178px** on some bands) — dramatic breath between technical blocks
- **16–24px** inner padding on denser modules
- Side margins ~156px on centered columns at large widths

### Radii and borders

- Radii **1–2px** — essentially sharp
- Borders **1–2px** solid defining panels and tables
- Shadows not part of the language

## Visual language

- Schematic / infrastructure aesthetic
- Code or browser-frame visuals as proof
- Flat planes, strict alignment
- Minimal softness

## Components and states

- Marketing nav with docs/GitHub style utilities
- Hero with oversized claim
- Logo/proof row
- Feature bands with technical diagrams
- Pricing tables/cards with sharp edges
- Dense comparison rows

## Responsive behavior

Eight captures of `/` and `/pricing` (~1392–1700px). Keep sharp geometry when stacking; display type scales down but should remain assertive.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**: sharp 1–2px radii, 2px rules, huge display, monochrome infrastructure feel, airy section padding.

**Avoid**: big soft 16px consumer cards, playful pastels, heavy drop shadows, or mascot illustration.

## Scope note

Eight captures home+pricing. Color roles unsupported in data; character relies on geometry metrics and screenshot reading.
