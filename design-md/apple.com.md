# How apple.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/apple.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Apple’s storefront is the textbook of **restrained premium commerce**: vast white space, near-black SF-like sans, one blue interactive accent, and photography that does the selling. UI chrome is almost invisible—thin utility bars, frosted controls, hairline rules—so product finishes and typography carry the brand.

What should survive adaptation:

- **White canvas + graphite text** (`#1d1d1f`) with secondary gray (`#6e6e73` / `#86868b`).
- **Single blue accent** `#0066cc` for links and primary text actions.
- Huge product imagery with minimal copy blocks.
- Centered content columns with large side margins.

## Foundations

### Color

Measured: text primary `#1d1d1f`, secondary `#6e6e73` / `#86868b` / `#000000`, accent `#0066cc`. Surfaces are white and light gray panels; dark product shots provide contrast. Avoid rainbow accents—Apple shop color is monochrome plus blue.

### Typography

Families not declared in capture (visually SF Pro / system sans). Sizes cluster around 12–17px body, ~24–40px headings, weight 400 body and 600 emphasis. Line-heights are open (e.g. 17/25, 24/28, 40/44).

### Spacing and layout

Very large horizontal margins (often 500px+ equivalent centering on wide viewports). Section padding 40–78px common. Content width feels intentionally narrow for readability beside full-bleed media.

## Visual language

- **Radius:** ~8–12px on buttons/tiles; ~18px on many shop tiles; full pills for some chips.
- **Borders:** 1px light gray hairlines; top rules under sticky bars.
- **Shadows:** scarce—frosted translucent bars and fills replace drop shadows.
- **Imagery:** studio product photography, seamless white or graphite fields.

## Components and states

- Global utility bar (store, search, bag) over content.
- Sticky local nav with frosted background.
- Primary blue text buttons / “Check Out” style actions; dark filled pills for some CTAs.
- Configuration grids and option tiles.
- Bag line items with thumbnail, price, stepper.
- Footer legal dense multi-column links.

## Responsive behavior

All captures are wide desktop (~2048). Apple’s known collapse patterns aren’t evidenced here—do not specify mobile breakpoints from this set alone.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** graphite-on-white type system, blue-only accents, heroic product media, generous centering.

**Avoid:** heavy card chrome, multi-color CTAs, dense marketplace grids, drop-shadow skeuomorphism.

## Scope note

Eight shop captures focused on MacBook Pro listing, configuration, and bag—not the marketing homepage. Typography families inferred visually only.
