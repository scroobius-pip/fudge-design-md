# How audio.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/audio.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

audio.com is a **dark creator audio platform**: deep charcoal/black fields, light primary text (`#f2f1f4`), muted gray secondary (`#67656d` / `#a7a5aa`), and hot magenta/pink CTAs (`#ff465f`) on marketing surfaces—with green (`#02ba29`) appearing as a success/plan accent on subscriptions. Floating white player mini-cards and waveform UI make the product feel audible even in stills.

What should survive adaptation:

- Dark canvas + light type + magenta primary CTA.
- Pill buttons (9999 radius) for main actions.
- Nearly sharp cards (1px radius) contrasting with soft pills.
- Player chips/cards that can float over the hero void.

## Foundations

### Color

Measured: accents `#ff465f` and `#02ba29`; text `#f2f1f4`; secondary `#67656d` / `#a7a5aa`. Keep magenta for join/try; green for positive/plan emphasis—not interchangeable.

### Typography

Sans 400/600. Body 16/24; titles 32/40; display ~50/64; small 12/16–20. Clear weight jumps for hierarchy.

### Spacing and layout

Common 24px horizontal padding; section 24–32 vertical. Centered hero with orbiting mini players. Pricing cards in bordered columns.

## Visual language

- Pills for CTAs and tags.
- 1px radius “almost square” cards—distinctive vs fully rounded SaaS.
- 1px borders around plan cards and inputs.
- Waveforms and progress bars as signature imagery.

## Components and states

- Top nav with Upload + Try for free.
- Hero headline + magenta pill CTA.
- Floating audio cards with artwork, title, tags.
- Sticky/mini player bar.
- Pricing tiers with feature lists.
- Tag chips for genres/topics.

## Responsive behavior

Many widths (964–1702). Hero remains dark centered; cards reflow. Exact breakpoints not tokenized.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** dark audio mood, magenta CTA pills, sharp-ish cards, floating players.

**Avoid:** light-mode default that loses club/studio vibe; mixing green and magenta as equal primary CTAs.

## Scope note

Home and subscriptions across eight captures. Font families undeclared.
