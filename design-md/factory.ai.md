# How factory.ai is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/factory.ai-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Factory is stark black enterprise-devtool marketing. Pages are near-void black with white type, hairline dividers, monochrome integration marks, and tiny orange status dots as rare warmth. Geometry is restrained (4–8px radii). The mood is serious, agent-native, security-aware — more terminal night mode than playful AI.

What should survive adaptation:
- True black canvas and white type
- Monochrome iconography for logos/integrations
- Small radii (4–8px); pills only where measured as full-round outliers
- Sparse sections separated by hairlines, not colorful cards

## Foundations

### Color

Structured roles unsupported. Visually:
- `#000000` canvas
- White primary type and key buttons
- Gray secondary labels
- Small orange dots for section status labels (“INTEGRATIONS”, “SECURITY”)
- White outline/secondary buttons on black

### Typography

Families unsupported. Observed:
- Body/UI ~16/19–24
- Mid ~18/21.6
- Display ~36/36 on company/enterprise claims

Tight, modern grotesque reading.

### Spacing and layout

- Vertical margins ~40–56px between blocks
- Padding 16–32px common; 24px very frequent
- Horizontal compactness with wide black field
- Enterprise page: left copy + right icon matrices

Radii: 8px and 4px primary.

## Visual language

Flat black, hairlines, monochrome partner grids, certificate/security icon rows. Almost no gradients in chrome. Depth is typographic and compositional.

## Components and states

- Top nav with LOG IN + CONTACT SALES
- Section label with orange dot + large title + short body + white button
- Integration icon matrix
- Security/compliance icon row
- Product/IDE and company pages reuse the black system

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** black void, monochrome marks, tiny orange dots, 4–8px controls.  
**Avoid** colorful AI meshes, glassmorphism, and large soft consumer pills.  
**Adaptation** for agent infrastructure, security-sensitive devtools, enterprise AI.
