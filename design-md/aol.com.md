# How aol.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/aol.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

AOL’s homepage is a **loud-friendly consumer portal**: a saturated yellow header carrying the bold black wordmark, a white search pill, and utility actions—then a white content canvas packed with news heroes, carousels, trending lists, and right-rail promos. It is intentionally denser and more mass-media than minimal SaaS marketing. The yellow bar is the brand handshake; everything below behaves like a modern content network.

What should survive adaptation:
- High-chroma yellow masthead with black AOL mark
- White content field with rounded news cards
- Hero carousel + trending modules + promo rail composition
- Friendly, high-legibility sans UI rather than luxury sparsity

Bold black wordmark; medium-heavy sans headlines on story cards; smaller muted meta; compact list titles in Trending. Hierarchy must stay scannable at a glance—portal users skim.

### Spacing and layout

Recurring spacing measurements include 5px, 20px, 40px, 56px, 130px, 165px. Dense multi-module grid: large left hero, accompanying story text, right promo stack, lower video/story rows. Gutters are tighter than premium lifestyle brands but still card-separated. Observed corner radii include 50px.

## Visual language

Rounded cards, soft shadows, carousel chevrons in circular buttons, thumbnail+headline rows, occasional illustrated promo tiles. The yellow header is flat and confident—no glass blur required.

## Components and states

- Yellow sticky/top header: menu, wordmark, search, help, mail, sign-in
- Secondary strip (Recently Visited / Local News, weather)
- Hero carousel with image + story panel + pagination
- Trending Now list with source avatars/thumbnails
- Promo modules / plan cards on the rail
- Video cards with play affordances

## Responsive behavior

Captures are wide desktop portals (~1711px). On smaller screens collapse rails under the hero, keep the yellow header compact, and maintain search prominence.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** yellow masthead authority, carded news density, scannable trending lists.  
**Avoid** stripping density into a single sparse SaaS hero that no longer feels like a portal; avoid muddy yellows.
