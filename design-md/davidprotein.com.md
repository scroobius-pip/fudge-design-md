# How davidprotein.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/davidprotein.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

David Protein is a **premium CPG ecommerce brand**: cinematic product photography, oversized display type (55–80px), and a dual mode of **dark immersive home** versus **light merchandising** (bundle builder, about, FAQ). A muted gold accent (`#b59f4a`) marks brand warmth on light commerce surfaces; white/`#dcdcdc` type rides photography on dark hero moments.

What should survive adaptation:

- **Product photography as the hero medium**, full-bleed and directional.
- **Huge calm display type** (often weight 400, not ultra-black shout).
- **Gold accent** `#b59f4a` on light shopping UI; white type on dark photo fields.
- Pill CTAs (1000px-class radii) and small **5px** card rounding on commerce tiles.
- Clean FAQ/about editorial pages with 45–50px titles and open padding.

## Foundations

### Color

| Role | Value | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dark-mode primary text | `#ffffff` | Type on photo heroes |
| Dark-mode secondary | `#dcdcdc` | Supporting lines on dark |
| Accent | `#b59f4a` | Gold brand accent on light commerce |
| Light primary text | `#000000` | Bundle/about/FAQ |
| Light secondary | `#6e6e6e` | Muted meta |

### Typography

Families not declared; bundle taxonomy notes serif presence for some headings. Measured:

- Display **80px** / 76.8px and **55px** / 60.5px on home; **40–50px** on commerce/about/FAQ.
- Body **14–16px** / ~19–24px; UI **12px** common on product meta.
- Mostly weight 400—confidence without heavy boxing.

### Spacing and layout

- Home dark sections: large **70px** padding blocks.
- Bundle builder: **40px** section pads, **15px** tile pads, ~**240px** side margins on wide frames.
- FAQ: **100px** vertical section padding, **57px** horizontal.
- About mobile crop still keeps 5px radii and 10–60px pads.

## Visual language

- Pills for primary actions; **5px** rounding on product tiles; dashed 1px borders appear on some bundle selection UI.
- Sparse shadows (soft 0/4/4 occasional); photography does the drama.
- Physical product renders and lifestyle crops dominate media.
- Motion not established.

## Components and states

### Home hero
Full-bleed product/lifestyle, giant headline, pill CTA, minimal nav.

### Build-a-bundle
Centered commerce column, selectable product tiles, gold accent on active/brand moments, clear secondary gray meta.

### About / FAQ
Editorial stacked sections, large titles, generous whitespace, simple text blocks.

## Responsive behavior

Widths from ~481px about crop through 1900–2048px home/bundle. Type scales down but stays large-display led; pills and 5px tiles persist.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**

1. Photo-first dark home vs light shop split.
2. Gold `#b59f4a` + black/gray on light; white type on dark photos.
3. Oversized weight-400 display.
4. Pill CTAs + small tile radii.
5. Airy FAQ/about pacing.

**Avoid**

1. Neon fitness-bro gradients.
2. Tiny dense grocery UI type on the brand home.
3. Dropping photography for abstract illustrations only.
4. Inventing font licences.

**Adaptation recipe**

- Cinematic product hero → giant quiet type → gold accent commerce → pill buy actions → calm FAQ.

## Scope note

Eight captures: home, build-a-bundle (×2), about (×2), FAQ. Supported: dual light/dark brand modes, gold accent, display scale, commerce tiles. Gaps: font family names, cart/checkout, motion.
