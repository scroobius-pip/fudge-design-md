# How drinkag1.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/drinkag1.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

AG1’s site is **premium wellness ecommerce** executed in a strict **black-and-white** foundation with photography doing the emotional work. Large product stills (canister, pouch, lifestyle) sit on clean fields; typography is bold, direct, and conversion-minded (“More than a greens powder”). Partner/get-started flows keep the same high-contrast discipline.

What should survive adaptation:

- Black/white as the system, not a rainbow health palette
- Hero-scale product photography
- Clear primary CTAs with pill/capsule geometry
- Sparse copy with strong claims

## Foundations

### Color

Measured:

- `#000000` and `#ffffff` as the dominant pair

Any greens live inside product photography/packaging, not as a full UI chrome color in the measured set. Keep UI monochrome and let the product package supply brand green.

### Typography

Declared families unknown. Display is large and confident; body is clean sans. Weights span regular to bold for claims vs details. Avoid playful script that undermines clinical-premium positioning.

### Spacing and layout

Radii **4 / 6 / 8 / 32 / 100**—controls may be slightly rounded rectangles while key CTAs go full pill (100). Home is a long marketing scroll of product chapters; partner pages compress to get-started sections. Wide desktop frames use cinematic product crops.

## Visual language

- Studio product photography, often centered
- High-contrast type blocks
- Minimal illustration; real product is the iconography
- Occasional lifestyle imagery still graded to fit monochrome UI chrome

## Components and states

- **Primary CTA:** dark or light filled pill depending on section ground
- **Nav:** light utility on white/black transparent variants
- **Product chapters:** image + short claim + CTA
- **Partner landing:** simplified get-started path

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**

- Monochrome UI + product photography color
- Pill CTAs
- Premium sparse copy

**Avoid**

- Neon supplement-store chaos
- Cluttered multi-primary buttons
- Muddy gray-on-gray text
