# How browseros.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/browseros.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

BrowserOS markets an open-source agentic browser with a **warm, editorial** tone rather than cold infra chrome. The canvas is soft ivory/off-white with a subtle grid texture, display lines mix **black roman and orange italic emphasis**, CTAs are dark brown/black pills, and product proof is a large rounded browser mockup floating over gentle photography.

What should survive adaptation:

- **Ivory paper canvas** + subtle grid
- **Orange accent** for emphasis words and active chips (`Browse` pill outline)
- **Serif/italic display moments** paired with clean sans body
- **Dark pill CTAs** (“Download BrowserOS”)
- **Large rounded product frame** (~24px) as the hero artifact

## Foundations

### Color

No structured roles; visual palette:

- Ivory / warm white background
- Near-black text
- Orange for emphasis and brand mark
- Dark brown/black fills for primary pills
- Soft sky/landscape photography inside mockups

### Typography

- Display ~**48–64px** with ~1.1–1.2 lh — mixed roman + italic orange words (“Agentic”, “Browser”)
- Body **16/20.8** regular
- Supporting **24/28.8**
- Overall more magazine than docs

### Spacing and layout

- Centered marketing column with ~**24–42px** horizontal padding
- Section tops ~**40px**; large bottom padding ~**128px** under hero stacks
- **10–32px** margins between stacked groups
- Hero: badge → display → body → email+download pill → secondary GitHub pill → product frame

### Radii and borders

- Product frames ~**24px**; chips/pills **100px**; smaller controls **12–18px**
- 1px borders on secondary pills and tab chips
- Soft, friendly geometry (opposite of Browserbase’s sharp 2px world)

## Visual language

- Warmth, lifestyle, product-in-scene
- YC badge and social icons as small trust marks
- Browser screenshot with agent side panel as the narrative proof
- Light grid texture in the background (visual)

## Components and states

- Top nav: mark, text links, dark GitHub pill
- Hero badge (“Backed by Combinator”)
- Mixed-emphasis headline
- Email field + dark download pill
- Ghost/secondary “Star on GitHub”
- Segmented pills (Browse / Extract / Research)
- Oversized rounded product preview
- Footer download repeat

## Responsive behavior

Eight home captures ~1254–1684px wide. Hero stays centered; product frame scales down; display type should shed size before losing the orange italic emphasis pattern.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve**: ivory canvas, orange italic emphasis, dark pills, 24px product radius, lifestyle mockup proof, editorial warmth.

**Avoid**: cold black developer-terminal skins, neon cyber accents, sharp 0-radius infra cards, or removing the serif/italic display contrast.

## Scope note

Eight homepage captures only. Color/type roles largely visual; radii and spacing metrics support the soft geometry claims.
