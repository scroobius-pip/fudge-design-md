# How evefrontier.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/evefrontier.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

EVE Frontier is dark, cinematic, and game-native. The canvas is near-black space; type sits in bone cream (`#fafae5`); the only hot accent is a searing orange-red (`#ff4700`). Layouts are vast — huge section padding, wide gaps, and media that feels like key art rather than UI chrome.

What should survive adaptation:
- True black void backgrounds
- Cream text instead of pure white (warmer, less sterile)
- Single orange accent for CTAs and critical highlights
- Expansive vertical padding (~120px) and ~80px gutters

## Foundations

### Color

Measured:
- Accent / brand hot: `#ff4700`
- Cream text/surface tint: `#fafae5`
- Black canvas: `#000000`

Relationships: black carries the world; cream carries reading; orange punctures for action. Avoid adding a second accent hue.

### Typography

Families not declared. Observed weights 300–400 at 14–16px for UI/body with 16–24px line-heights. Display and marketing titles are visually much larger in the key art compositions (game trailer energy). Taxonomy labels mark display/body roles and sans-serif usage.

### Spacing and layout

Dominant measurements:
- Section padding 120px top/bottom, 80px left/right
- Column/gap 80px
- Wide auto side margins on centered shells (~393px on some frames)

This is billboard spacing, not dashboard density.

## Visual language

- Minimal radii in chrome; focus is full-bleed art
- Little reliance on cards; scenes and typography do the work
- Gradients may appear inside key art, not as UI skins
- Taxonomy: entertainment/gaming, image background, large type, orange hue

## Components and states

- Sparse top nav over dark art
- Primary orange CTA
- Language path `/en` marketing shell
- Feature bands that alternate art and short copy

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** black + cream + `#ff4700`, and the huge quiet gaps.  
**Avoid** light mode, multicolored badges, and tight SaaS card grids.  
**Adaptation** for AAA-adjacent game marketing and dark cinematic product stories.

## Scope note

Four captures, all `/en` marketing frames. Structured color roles present; font families unsupported. Responsive matrix limited to desktop-like widths.
