# How aaa24.a24films.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/aaa24.a24films.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

AAA24 is A24’s **membership and account world** — more boutique club than neon streaming store. The system mixes warm paper neutrals (`#f5f1ea`) with muted stone grays (`#837e77`), big photography, and calm typography. It feels tactile and editorial, like print collateral extended to the web.

What should survive adaptation:
- Warm off-white / paper grounds
- Stone-gray secondary text
- Cinematic stills and object photography
- Quiet, confident subscription cards

## Foundations

### Color

Measured:
- Stone gray: `#837e77`
- Paper / cream: `#f5f1ea`
- Black ink for primary headlines and primary buttons (visually)
- Photography supplies the saturated color

### Typography

No declared family captured; the voice mixes sturdy display with readable body.
- Large display and section titles spanning roughly **mid-30s to 60px+** in places; many UI sizes **13–18px**
- Body around **14–16.8px** with line-heights ~1.2–1.3
- Mostly weight 400 in captured styles — emphasis via size and case more than black weight

### Spacing and layout

- Vertical section gaps often **30–75px**
- Card padding comfortable; subscription layouts use clear two-up cards on desktop
- Negative micro-margins appear in optical adjustments — prefer intentional alignment over mechanical 8pt dogma
- Radii are subtle where present; not a heavy pill language on every control

## Visual language

- Film stills, product photography (totes, magazines), soft shadows optional
- Cream panels on darker cinematic bands
- Serif or high-taste display moments for campaign lines (visually), with simple sans UI
- Membership cards with illustration/photo header + price + select CTA

## Components and states

1. Top marketing nav + Subscribe  
2. Campaign hero with photography  
3. Plan cards (Digital / Premium style modules)  
4. FAQ / account utility pages with quieter type  
5. Auth/not-found frames that keep the same neutral ground  

Primary buttons read solid dark on light cards; secondary text stays stone gray.

## Responsive behavior

Home, FAQs, auth, and account-check routes across wide desktop captures. Stack plan cards on narrow viewports; keep cream/stone pairing rather than flipping to pure white SaaS.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** paper cream, stone gray, cinematic photos, calm plan cards.  
**Avoid:** neon streamer UI, heavy glassmorphism, generic blue CTAs.

## Scope note

Eight captures across `/`, FAQs, auth, and account-check. Colors measured; type sizes observed; exact font family names not declared.
