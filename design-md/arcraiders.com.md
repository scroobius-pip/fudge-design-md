# How arcraiders.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/arcraiders.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

ARC Raiders is **AAA game marketing chrome**: dark metallic environments, cream/off-white body type, and a hot gold accent (`#f1aa1c`) for CTAs and highlights. Type is loud—condensed or heavy display at 64–80px—set against cinematic key art. The UI feels tactical and industrial rather than friendly SaaS.

What should survive adaptation:

- Dark canvas + cream text (`#ece2d0`) + white secondary + gold accent.
- Oversized display headlines with tight-to-solid line-height.
- Wide horizontal padding (40–160px) and tall section bands (80px).
- Sharp, slightly aggressive buttons (small radii ~4–5px, thicker 2px borders).

## Foundations

### Color

Measured: accent `#f1aa1c`, text primary `#ece2d0`, secondary `#ffffff`. Ground is near-black with environmental art. Gold is reserved for commitment and brand sparks.

### Typography

Sans/display mix; weights 400/600/700. Sizes: 16 body, 20–32 mid, 64–80 display (sometimes line-height ≤ font-size for poster tension).

### Spacing and layout

Creator pages use very large side padding (160px) and vertical section padding 80px. Content is banded full-width over art, not a narrow blog column.

## Visual language

- Small radii (4–5px) — not bubbly.
- 2px solid borders on key controls.
- Key art and illustration carry emotion; UI stays utilitarian.

## Components and states

- Top game nav / platform badges.
- Gold or high-contrast CTAs (Join, Watch, etc.).
- Feature bands with icon + short copy.
- News/patch article layouts.
- Footer legal/platform links.

## Responsive behavior

Multiple widths captured (roughly 1166–1712). Layout remains dark full-bleed marketing; detailed mobile nav patterns not fully specified.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** gold-on-dark tactical palette, poster display type, industrial tightness of controls.

**Avoid:** pastel SaaS restyles, large 16px+ “friendly” radii, light-mode default without strong art direction.

## Scope note

Mix of creator program, home, and news captures. Several older home captures lack structured tokens; strongest measurements from creator-program pages.
