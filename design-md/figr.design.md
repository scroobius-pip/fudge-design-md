# How figr.design is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/figr.design-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Figr is a soft, premium AI design-agent brand. Pages sit on a warm off-white/cream canvas with elegant black serif display at very large sizes (~76px, weight 300), calm sans UI, and lavender/purple pill CTAs. Contact uses a split layout: big serif question on the left, spacious form on the right. It feels boutique and human, not cyberpunk AI.

What should survive adaptation:
- Cream/off-white canvas (not stark cold white)
- Light-weight huge serif display (~76/91 at 300)
- Lavender filled pills for primary actions
- 24px large radii on cards/hero media; 8px on smaller controls
- Generous top padding (~128px) and 32px horizontal padding

## Foundations

### Color

Structured roles unsupported. Visually:
- Cream page background
- Black display/body text
- Lavender/purple primary buttons and signup pills
- Light gray form fields and dashed upload zone
- Soft gray secondary buttons (“Book a demo”)

### Typography

Families unsupported. Observed:
- Display ~76px weight 300, line-height ~91
- Body ~16/25.6
- Small ~13/25.6
- Serif for big marketing lines; sans for nav/forms

### Spacing and layout

- Horizontal padding 32px
- Top padding 128px on major hero/contact bands
- 16px stacks inside forms
- 48px bottom padding on some sections
- Margin bottom 16; margin top 32

Radii: 24px prominent, 8px secondary.

## Visual language

Soft and airy. Minimal hard borders; fields use light fills/outlines. Home pairs serif claims with product visuals; contact is editorial split. Footer can flip to black band under the cream page.

## Components and states

- Nav: wordmark, text links, Login, soft “Book a demo”, lavender “Sign up for free”
- Hero with huge serif headline
- Contact split: serif prompt + form (subject, description, upload, email, lavender Submit)
- Cards/sections at 24px radius

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** cream canvas, light huge serif, lavender pills, airy forms.  
**Avoid** dark neon AI aesthetics and heavy bold-only type systems.  
**Adaptation** for design tools, agencies, and “human AI” products.

## Scope note

Two captures: home and contact. Enough for brand chassis; pricing/product deep dives not included. Colors/fonts from visual reading + spacing/type measurements.
