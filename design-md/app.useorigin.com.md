# How app.useorigin.com is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/app.useorigin.com-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

Origin Financial pairs a **premium dark brand moment** with a **clean light product shell**. Marketing/sign-up screens use deep navy-black fields, oversized editorial headlines, and a crisp cyan wordmark. Inside the app, the system flips to white surfaces, slate text, soft cards, and the same cyan as the interactive accent—finance UI that feels modern rather than bank-beige.

What should survive adaptation:

- Cyan (`#00b3dd`) as the signature accent on both dark and light canvases.
- **Split-panel acquisition** layouts (brand story left, form right).
- Large, confident display type for money and milestones.
- Soft 8–16px rounded cards on a light product canvas.

## Foundations

### Color

Measured roles on sign-up: accent `#00b3dd`, primary text `#ffffff` (on dark), secondary text `#01000a`. Product surfaces are white/off-white with dark body text; cyan fills primary buttons and key highlights. Avoid inventing additional semantic state colors beyond what screens show (standard green/red for market deltas may appear in charts).

### Typography

Declared families were not captured. Visually: geometric sans for UI; very large display sizes (~48–96px) for hero/onboarding headlines; 14–16px body with ~20–24px line-height; medium weights for section titles.

### Spacing and layout

Sign-up uses a hard vertical split. Product home uses a left sidebar + main column with 16–24px card padding, 16px radii common on marketing cards and 8px on denser controls. Horizontal padding on onboarding often 24–48px.

## Visual language

- **Radius:** 16px on large cards/panels; 8px and 4px on smaller controls.
- **Borders:** 1px solid subtle dividers; cards often borderless with fill contrast.
- **Shadows:** minimal in captures—depth mostly from surface color.
- **Imagery:** abstract gradients/orbs on brand panels; simple iconography in-app.

## Components and states

- Wordmark lockup in cyan on dark.
- Primary filled cyan buttons; secondary outline/ghost on light.
- Sidebar nav with icon + label.
- Portfolio/summary cards with large numeric figures.
- Form fields with light fills and clear labels.
- Onboarding checklist / progress content blocks.

## Responsive behavior

Captures include wide desktop (~2048) and a narrower onboarding frame (~776). The split sign-up and sidebar shell are desktop-first; narrow onboarding stacks as a single column card.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve:** cyan signature, dark↔light brand/product contrast, oversized money typography, soft card radii.

**Avoid:** muddy blue substitutes for cyan; cluttered multi-accent dashboards; tight banking-form density on marketing surfaces.

## Scope note

Three pages: sign-up, home preview, free-trial onboarding. Font family names not declared in the capture set.
