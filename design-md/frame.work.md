# How frame.work is designed

[Open the live Fudge conversation](https://design.withfudge.com/share/frame.work-design)

Last updated: 2026-07-28

## Design character

frame.work (Framework) sells modular laptops with a **clean hardware-brand system**: light canvas, confident sans headlines (~48px/600), product photography as hero, and long-scrolling spec storytelling. It feels precise and engineer-honest — generous side margins (~132px outer), 40px content padding, and pill CTAs — closer to premium consumer electronics than startup gradient landings.

What should survive adaptation:

- Light canvas + sharp product photography.
- Strong sans display at ~48/60 with 16/24 body.
- Wide margins and section padding (24–80px vertical bands).
- Pill primary actions; minimal decorative chrome.
- Modular product truth over lifestyle fluff.

## Foundations

### Color

No structured roles. Visual:

| Role | Approx | Use |
|------|--------|-----|
| Canvas | `#ffffff` / off-white | Page |
| Ink | `#0a0a0a` | Headlines, body |
| Muted | `#5c5c5c` | Secondary |
| Fill control | light gray / black pill | Buttons |

### Typography

| Role | Size | Weight | LH |
|------|------|--------|-----|
| Body | 16px | 400 | 24px |
| Body alt | 16px | 400 | ~28px |
| Display | 48px | 600 | 60px |
| UI emph | 16px | 600 | 24px |

### Spacing and layout

- Outer horizontal margin ~**132px** with inner **40px** padding common.
- Section vertical padding 24–80px.
- Single product narrative page (`/laptop13pro`) — long scroll, media + copy bands.

## Visual language

- Rectilinear product shots; full-pill controls.
- Little shadow theatrics; photography carries depth.
- Spec grids and feature rows in even columns.

## Components and states

1. Global marketing nav + buy.
2. Product hero with headline + dual CTAs.
3. Feature bands (photo + copy).
4. Spec / comparison blocks.
5. Footer utility links.

## Responsive behavior

Widths ~1186–1621px. Wide margins tighten on smaller boards; scale display before collapsing margins to 24–40px.

## Practical guidance

**Preserve** hardware clarity, wide quiet margins, 48/16 type pairing, pill buy actions.  
**Avoid** neon AI gradients, tiny dense admin type, cluttered badge salad.
