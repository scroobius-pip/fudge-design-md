## Overview

Adfects is a clean selling page for ad design services. The visual language is spare at first glance: white canvas, black type, thin gray rules, and dark rounded call-to-action bars. The page does not try to feel like a dashboard. It feels like a polished studio landing page where the work samples do the heavy lifting.

The strongest pattern is the contrast between the calm page structure and the colorful creative mockups. The hero combines a large headline with a grid of ad cards. The pricing section turns into a straightforward two-card comparison. The footer opens into a soft blurred collage with logo, navigation, and social links layered over floating sample cards. That progression gives the page a clear rhythm without adding extra ornament.

The design should preserve four things above all: strong black typography, generous white space, hairline separators, and the floating ad-artwork mood. Color stays disciplined. The interface is mostly monochrome, while lilac, rose, and lime appear as accent glows inside the creative imagery rather than as global chrome.

## Colors

The color system is intentionally narrow. White and near-white surfaces carry almost everything. Black anchors headings, labels, and small marks. Deep navy is reserved for dark call-to-action bars and the strongest interactive surfaces. Gray sits between those extremes for separators, helper copy, and quiet metadata. The accent colors are present, but only as image tint, blur color, or small creative highlights.

| token | value | use |
|---|---|---|
| `canvas` | `#fbfdfc` | Main page background and the broad white breathing room around sections |
| `surface` | `#f6f8f8` | Soft panels, translucent footer fields, and quiet card backdrops |
| `surface-muted` | `#c9c9ce` | Pale separators, subdued fills, and the lightest structural lines |
| `border` | `#a6a8ab` | Hairline dividers and understated card edges |
| `ink` | `#000000` | Logo text, headlines, pricing figures, and high-contrast copy |
| `muted-ink` | `#656272` | Body copy, menu links, plan notes, and copyright text |
| `action` | `#000000` | Primary button bodies, dark plan bars, and the heaviest interaction surfaces |
| `action-soft` | `#20222e` | Secondary dark fills and deeper button shadow tones |
| `accent-lilac` | `#7b73c8` | Purple glow inside card art and the soft colored haze in the footer collage |
| `accent-rose` | `#e75054` | Pink-red glow inside the artwork and small highlight marks |
| `accent-lime` | `#8daa38` | Bright green accent inside the sample cards and image overlays |

The page should read as light-first. Dark fills are tactical, not atmospheric. A dark CTA bar stands out against the white field, but the rest of the interface stays open. The accent colors never take over the layout; they sit inside the creative pieces so the page stays clear and brand-forward instead of turning into a colored poster.

## Typography

Instrument Sans carries the display hierarchy. It suits the homepage because the forms are round enough for a friendly sales page but still direct enough for product marketing. Inter handles the quieter work: navigation, pricing notes, metadata, legal text, and the link columns in the footer. The pairing keeps the page crisp and modern without introducing a second decorative voice.

Instrument Sans is credited to Rodrigo Fuenzalida and Frag Type. Inter is credited to Rasmus Andersson and Rsms. Reuse should confirm the font license before shipping a final product.

| token | family | size | weight | leading | tracking | use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `hero-display` | Instrument Sans | 4.5rem | 700 | 0.95 | -0.04em | Large landing-page headline |
| `section-display` | Instrument Sans | 3rem | 700 | 0.98 | -0.035em | Section titles and pricing intro text |
| `pricing-price` | Instrument Sans | 2.5rem | 700 | 1 | -0.03em | Monthly price figures and other numeric emphasis |
| `body` | Inter | 1rem | 400 | 1.5 | 0em | Supporting paragraphs and explanatory copy |
| `body-medium` | Inter | 1rem | 500 | 1.45 | 0em | Buttons, emphasized body text, and strong labels |
| `navigation` | Inter | 0.95rem | 500 | 1.3 | 0em | Header links and compact menu items |
| `label` | Inter | 0.8125rem | 500 | 1.2 | 0.08em | Small uppercase section tags and category marks |
| `legal-copy` | Inter | 0.8125rem | 400 | 1.4 | 0em | Copyright, policy links, and footer fine print |

The scale is driven more by weight and spacing than by a long ladder of sizes. Display text is bold and tight. Supporting text opens up just enough to stay readable in wide columns. Labels use tracking to create contrast without needing a heavier weight. Prices sit between headline and body: large enough to sell, restrained enough to keep the cards from overpowering the page.

## Layout

The page uses a centered, wide-column structure with large white margins and thin structural lines. That grid is visible in the pricing section, where the left introduction sits beside two tall cards, and again in the footer, where the logo block, menu column, and links column all line up across the same broad frame. The layout never feels dense. Empty space is part of the composition, not leftover area.

The hero is a two-part stage. Copy is left aligned and stacked tightly: a short kicker, a large multi-line headline, a brief supporting sentence, and a pair of adjacent actions. The creative board on the right balances that text with a grid of tilted ad thumbnails. The board should feel curated, almost like pinned sample work, not like a catalog.

The pricing section keeps the same calm spacing but simplifies the message. The left column explains the offer in plain language. The plan cards on the right are tall, narrow, and easy to compare. Their structure is repetitive on purpose: plan name, price, short note, a dark CTA bar, and a short included-items list. That repetition lets the difference in price and scope land quickly.

The footer changes tempo without changing the grid discipline. The top of the footer is a blurred collage of sample cards floating over a pale haze. Beneath that, the logo, menu, and links continue in neat columns with quiet metadata and social buttons. The result is soft and layered, but still structurally simple.

Spacing should stay generous at section edges and tighter inside cards. The page works because it keeps a consistent rhythm: broad outer gutters, medium inter-panel spacing, then compact stacks inside each card or text block. Hairline borders can do a lot of the separation work here, so the layout does not need heavy shadows or thick frames.

## Visual language

Adfects uses a studio-like visual language rather than a product-dashboard language. The page feels built from presentation boards, ad examples, and clean selling copy. The typography is assertive, but the surrounding structure is light. That balance keeps the page from feeling corporate or overdesigned.

The most distinctive visual move is the floating-card treatment. Ad mockups tilt slightly, overlap the page edges, and sit above very soft shadows. In the footer collage, the blur washes the cards together into one misty band, which makes the lower page feel atmospheric without hiding the content. The colored glows are subtle and never fully separate from the white field. Lilac, rose, and lime act like studio lights on the work, not like page-wide theme colors.

The shapes are mostly rounded rectangles with measured corner radii. Buttons and plan bars are smoother and denser than the cards around them, which helps the calls to action read as clickable. Hairline borders and light divider rules keep the white areas from dissolving into one blank field. The overall effect is calm, sharp, and slightly promotional, with just enough softness in the artwork to keep the page human.

## Components

### Header and utility nav

- **Anatomy:** compact logo at left, a short row of text links, and a small rounded action button at the far right.
- **Typography:** Inter navigation text with medium weight and modest size.
- **Surface:** sits directly on the white canvas with almost no framing.
- **Composition:** the header is slim and understated so the hero headline stays dominant.
- **Visible state:** the action button is the most solid element in the row, while the text links stay quiet and light.

### Hero stage

- **Anatomy:** kicker, oversized headline, one support line, two adjacent buttons, and a right-side grid of ad mockups.
- **Typography:** Instrument Sans for the headline; Inter for the kicker, support line, and buttons.
- **Surface:** white background with thin structure and soft floating artwork.
- **Shape:** rounded corners appear in the cards, the buttons, and the little mark tiles, but not everywhere.
- **Composition:** text and creative samples share the width evenly; the left side sells the service, the right side proves it.
- **Visible states:** the dark primary button is the strongest action surface; the lighter secondary button stays understated.

### Pricing cards

- **Anatomy:** section intro on the left, two tall plan cards on the right, each with price, note, CTA bar, and included list.
- **Typography:** Instrument Sans for the plan price; Inter for labels, notes, and list items.
- **Surface:** white cards on white canvas, separated by hairlines and vertical rhythm.
- **Shape:** cards are softly rounded but still rectangular enough to read as comparison panels.
- **Spacing:** the CTA bar sits below the price with a deliberate gap, then the includes list follows in a compact stack.
- **Visible states:** the dark plan bar is the primary click target and the visual weight of each card.

### Footer collage

- **Anatomy:** logo and copyright block, menu column, links column, social buttons, and floating creative cards above a misty overlay.
- **Typography:** Inter for all footer text, with stronger weight only on small headings.
- **Surface:** pale blur and white haze over the creative strip, then clean text columns below.
- **Composition:** the top strip feels like a mood board; the lower text area feels like a utility footer.
- **Visible states:** the small square social buttons are bright, compact, and tactile against the lighter background.

### Social buttons and small icon tiles

- **Anatomy:** square tiles with simple monochrome marks.
- **Surface:** white or near-white fill with a faint edge and soft shadow.
- **Shape:** compact rounded corners, not pill shapes.
- **Hierarchy:** these sit low in the page hierarchy and should never compete with the main CTA bars.
- **Use:** they break up the footer’s text-heavy area and add a little tactile finish.

## Responsive behavior

On narrower widths, the order should remain the same: headline, support text, primary action, creative samples, then pricing, then footer. The hero board should stack before it becomes unreadably compressed, and the pricing cards should move from side-by-side comparison to a clear vertical sequence. The footer collage can collapse into a simpler stacked strip so the blur and the floating cards do not turn into noise.

The typography should step down in a controlled way rather than jumping. The display headline can lose width and a little size, but it should keep its dense, compact feel. Buttons should remain large enough to feel deliberate, with enough space around the text for the dark bars to stay legible. Hairline borders and thin separators can survive the shrink well, but only if the page keeps enough outer padding.

## Practical implementation guidance

### Preserve

- Keep the page light-first with black type and dark CTA bars.
- Keep the ad mockups floating, slightly tilted, and separated from the main copy block.
- Keep the footer collage soft and layered instead of turning it into a flat link farm.
- Keep the structure disciplined: broad page gutters, thin dividers, and compact stacks inside cards.
- Keep the accent colors inside the work samples and footer haze, not across the whole interface.

### Avoid

- Avoid replacing the dark CTA bars with outline buttons; the density of the dark fill is part of the page’s selling power.
- Avoid adding broad color bands, heavy gradients, or large tinted panels outside the artwork.
- Avoid over-rounding every shape. The page needs a mix of soft cards and tighter utility elements.
- Avoid thick shadows on every component; the design depends on restraint.
- Avoid making the footer look like a second hero. It should feel atmospheric, then functional.

### Recommended build order

1. Set the canvas, gutters, and thin divider system.
2. Install the Instrument Sans and Inter hierarchy.
3. Build the header and hero copy column.
4. Add the tilted mockup grid with soft shadows.
5. Reuse the same card shell for the pricing plans.
6. Finish the footer collage, link columns, and social tiles.
7. Tighten the mobile stack so the narrative order stays intact.

### Accessibility

- Keep black text on white surfaces wherever possible, and use the dark fills only where light text remains clear.
- Give the dark CTA bars a strong focus ring that does not disappear into the navy fill.
- Provide alt text for the mockup cards that names the layout and the ad message, not just the frame.
- Preserve readable line length in the hero and pricing intro when the column narrows.
- Make the footer link columns easy to scan with enough vertical spacing and a clear hover or focus cue.

## Scope note

This guide covers the adfects homepage hero, pricing block, and footer collage. Measurements are practical adaptation targets. Mobile stacking details, motion, and fine interaction states are not included here.
