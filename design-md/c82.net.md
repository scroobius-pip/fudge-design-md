## Overview

c82.net presents the Naturalist’s Library as a disciplined reading environment for plates, captions, and index browsing. The design feels closer to a museum catalog or a printed natural-history volume than to a modern app. The visual language depends on a narrow set of materials: paper-white and cream grounds, an evergreen masthead, burnt-orange links and buttons, black reading text, and oversized display lettering that makes the site title feel carved rather than typed.

The system is built around contrast between quiet surfaces and emphatic labels. The cream background keeps the illustration plates and volume cards calm, while the dark green header and the green home hero establish the site’s strongest blocks. Burnt orange carries the user’s action path, including navigation emphasis, category tabs, and utility links. The illustrations remain the emotional center; the interface stays light enough that the plates can carry the character of each page.

The page family is coherent because the same ingredients repeat with little variation. The header uses a flat dark band. The home hero uses a large green field with white stacked title type. The index uses cream paper with orange category tabs and a dense grid of plate cards. The detail view gives an illustration nearly full-page space beside a compact text column. The bottom promotional strip returns to green again and closes with a white legal bar. Nothing here depends on ornamental chrome or layered interface depth.

## Colors

The palette is small and role-based. The design does not need many hues because the illustration art already brings variety. Light surfaces do the reading work; dark evergreen blocks frame the brand; orange marks the places where the user can act.

| token | hex | use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#000000` | Primary text on paper surfaces, rules, and dense labels |
| `deep moss` | `#495945` | Masthead band, home hero field, and promotional hero background |
| `moss` | `#73976A` | Large title fill, green link tone, and lighter evergreen emphasis |
| `action` | `#DD6523` | Buttons, active tabs, section links, and utility actions |
| `paper` | `#F2EFE9` | Main reading canvas and the base behind the catalog grid |
| `paper-soft` | `#F8F7F5` | Slightly brighter paper tone for quiet separation and lifted cards |
| `canvas` | `#FFFFFF` | Clean framing space, footer bar, and the brightest card surface |

Light and dark here are chapter markers, not separate themes. The light paper field carries the index and the detail reading area. The dark evergreen band is reserved for the masthead, the home intro, and the book-promo block. Orange remains constant across both modes, so the eye always knows where the interactive path sits. The illustrations themselves act like a third mode: they sit on white or cream ground, but their colored plates and graphite lines bring enough contrast that the interface can stay restrained.

The accent color should never become decorative noise. It works best when it is rare and directional: a tab that shows the current category, a link that points to a volume or location, or a button that asks the visitor to move forward. The rest of the palette should support paper, reading, and image clarity rather than compete with them.

## Typography

The type system is unusually legible for a display-heavy archive because it relies on one calm reading family and one theatrical title family. **Alegreya Sans** carries the body text, labels, and navigation. **Rigsolidfill**, **Rigsolidinlinesolo**, and **Rigsolidreverse** create the site’s carved, stacked, museum-poster title language. The packet also contains **Arial**, **Applesystem**, and **Times** as fallback families; they do not define the brand voice. Font licensing is not supplied.

| token | family | size | weight | leading | tracking | use |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `hero-display` | Rigsolidfill | 5.2452rem | 700 | 1 | 0em | Home title and the strongest page-level headings |
| `hero-outline` | Rigsolidinlinesolo | 4.1962rem | 400 | 1 | 0em | Outlined title treatment and alternate hero wording |
| `hero-reverse` | Rigsolidreverse | 4.1962rem | 400 | 0.9 | 0em | Inverted title treatment in dark promotional bands |
| `body` | Alegreya Sans | 1.375rem | 400 | 1.6 | 0em | Detail paragraphs, explanatory copy, and page reading text |
| `body-compact` | Alegreya Sans | 1.2375rem | 400 | 1.6 | 0em | Tabs, inline links, captions, and short metadata lines |
| `button` | Arial | 1.2375rem | 400 | 1 | 0.07em | Small action buttons and utility controls |
| `browser-ui` | Applesystem | 0.875rem | 400 | 1 | 0em | Inherited browser chrome; not part of the brand system |
| `fallback-roman` | Times | 1.375rem | 400 | 1.6 | 0em | Default serif fallback when the intended stack drops through |

The hierarchy is built from scale rather than from many weights. Display type is large, tight, and often outlined or filled with a strong internal shadow effect. Body copy is generous, with an easy reading rhythm that suits long descriptive text and catalog notes. The tabs and utility links sit between those extremes: not tiny, but clearly subordinate to the plates and headings. The overall feeling is editorial, not app-like.

## Layout

The layout follows a page rhythm that alternates between framed blocks and open reading space. The masthead is a narrow dark band with compact horizontal padding of 1.375rem vertically and 2.75rem horizontally. Beneath it, the home hero expands into a broad green panel with the title centered or slightly offset, a short supporting paragraph, and a prominent orange action. The page then yields to a cream catalog area where the eye can scan category tabs before dropping into a wide grid of specimen cards.

Spacing is generous and repetitive. The main layout rhythm uses a 2.75rem gutter, and the large content sections open with about 6.45rem of vertical padding. That repeatable spacing matters because the pages are information dense: they combine navigation, explanation, illustration, and metadata. The layout keeps that density from feeling crowded by giving each material a stable field.

The index grid is especially important. It arranges the volumes as a broad matrix of tall cards, each one acting like a plate cover. The cards are tightly aligned, evenly spaced, and visually uniform, which lets the illustrations do the differentiation. The grid has enough columns to feel archival rather than editorial magazine-like. It is a catalog wall, not a marketing strip.

The detail page uses a different balance. One side gives the illustration nearly the full height of the viewport; the other side carries a narrow text block with a category line, a large green title, the italicized scientific name, a descriptive paragraph, and small linked metadata rows. That asymmetry is central to the experience. The image is not a thumbnail for the text; the text is the annotation beside the image.

The closing promotional band returns to a two-column structure: big message on one side, product or book render on the other. The final white footer strip reduces contrast and compresses the hierarchy into legal and credit information. The sequence feels like a printed book’s front matter, contents, plate, and colophon rather than a homepage, listing page, and footer stack.

The smaller internal gaps repeat the same scale language. Card groups and tab rows hold to roughly 0.6875rem spacing, captions breathe with a small 0.34375rem rhythm, and button padding stays compact enough to read as a control rather than a block. That consistency keeps the interface quiet while the illustrations do the loud work.

## Visual language

The site’s visual language is spare, but not plain. Its personality comes from the tension between formal typography and hand-colored plates. The illustration cards retain visible sketch lines, pale backgrounds, and atmospheric darkening near the bottom so the titles remain readable. That fading treatment gives the cards a slight exhibition label quality: the image is the subject, and the type is pinned over it.

Borders stay simple. The site relies on straight edges and square corners, which reinforces the archival tone. There is no appetite for soft app geometry or floating pills. Where elevation exists, it is hard and literal: a shadow offset to the lower right, with no blur, making the cards feel printed on paper and lifted slightly from the canvas. This is a crisp, physical kind of depth rather than a soft digital one.

The display type has its own material character. The filled Rigsolid variant feels carved and dense; the inline and reverse variants feel like poster treatments or hand-set sign painting. That makes the home title and section banners feel ceremonial. They are not generic headings. They are large labels for a reference collection.

Color and texture are also disciplined. Orange is warm and specific, never neon. Green is muted and historical, not fresh or botanical in a modern brand sense. The paper whites are tinted enough to feel aged, which suits the natural-history subject. The art itself supplies the richest palette, so the interface stays calm and mostly monochrome around it.

The whole system depends on contrast between weight and restraint. Large display type is paired with calm body text. Dark fields are paired with pale paper. Ornamental plates are paired with spare framing. That balance lets the archive feel scholarly without becoming sterile.

## Components

### Masthead

- **Anatomy:** Site title at left, horizontal navigation across the top, occasional selected tab or highlighted item.
- **Surface:** A flat deep-green band with white text.
- **Typography:** The title uses the largest Rigsolid treatment; navigation uses Alegreya Sans.
- **Shape:** Square corners; no capsule treatment.
- **Spacing:** Tight vertical padding, with broad horizontal breathing room.
- **Composition:** The title is a brand marker, but it does not overpower the content below. Navigation remains clear and evenly spaced.

### Home hero

- **Anatomy:** Large brand title, centered or near-centered supporting line, short explanatory paragraph, and a primary orange action.
- **Surface:** Evergreen field with subtle tonal texture behind the title.
- **Typography:** White or light title treatment, then smaller body copy in a readable sans face.
- **Composition:** Large headline first, then a concise explanation, then action. The book object on the right balances the text block and keeps the hero from feeling text-heavy.
- **Visible states:** The selected nav item is boxed or muted against the dark band, while the main action stays orange and highly legible.

### Category tabs

- **Anatomy:** A row of text tabs that switch between volume groups.
- **Surface:** Paper background with one active orange tab and inactive pale tabs.
- **Typography:** Compact Alegreya Sans, with the active tab set in white or high-contrast text.
- **Shape:** Rectangular tabs, square corners, and even spacing.
- **Hierarchy:** The active category is obvious without extra icons or heavy borders.
- **Composition:** Tabs sit above the grid and establish the browsing frame before the cards begin.

### Volume cards

- **Anatomy:** Tall cover-like tile, illustration, large numeric index, title text, and a bottom fade that protects readability.
- **Surface:** White or near-white card with a hard shadow offset down and right.
- **Typography:** Big numeric marker, then shorter title lines in a compact sans face.
- **Shape:** Straight edges and square corners.
- **Spacing:** Consistent card gaps and even internal padding; the title sits in the lower third.
- **Visible states:** The card’s readability depends on the bottom fade, not on a separate text panel.

### Illustration detail plate

- **Anatomy:** Full illustration on one side, explanatory text block on the other, followed by metadata links.
- **Surface:** Cream or paper ground, with black reading text and orange links.
- **Typography:** Large green title, italic scientific name, then a longer paragraph in Alegreya Sans.
- **Composition:** The illustration gets the widest share of space; the text column stays narrow and disciplined.
- **Visible states:** Linked place and contents entries appear as underlined or color-coded actions rather than as buttons.

### Promotional band and footer strip

- **Anatomy:** Large selling message, product image, short legal and credit line, and a small right-aligned utility link cluster.
- **Surface:** Dark green promo block followed by a white footer bar.
- **Typography:** Big white display text in the band; smaller paper or black legal text below.
- **Composition:** The image balances the message rather than decorating it.
- **Hierarchy:** The band carries one call to action; the footer reduces emphasis and closes the page.

## Responsive behavior

On narrower screens, the sequence should stay intact: masthead, hero statement, category tabs, grid of plates, then detail annotation and footer. The page can reflow from wide columns into stacked blocks, but it should not lose the sense that the plates are primary and the labels are secondary. The green header and orange actions need to remain distinct even when the layout compresses. The image cards should keep their bottom fade when card height changes. The detail view should preserve the image-first balance rather than collapsing the text on top of the artwork.

When space tightens, reduce column count before reducing type contrast. Keep the hierarchy visible: title, explanatory copy, action, then supporting metadata. The archive voice depends on calm spacing more than on a rigid grid.

## Practical implementation guidance

### Preserve

- Keep the paper field warm and slightly tinted. Pure white alone is too sterile for this subject.
- Use one strong orange action tone everywhere actions matter.
- Let the display type stay large and formal; it carries much of the site’s character.
- Retain the hard offset shadow on cards if elevation is needed.
- Keep the cards square and archival rather than pill-shaped or glassy.
- Treat illustration plates as the main content, not as decoration around a text product.

### Avoid

- Avoid introducing a second accent color that competes with the orange links.
- Avoid soft, blurred shadows; they weaken the print-like feel.
- Avoid rounded corners on tabs, cards, and plates.
- Avoid tiny body text. The reading experience depends on generous sizing and relaxed leading.
- Avoid dense interface chrome, icons, and framed controls that would make the catalog feel like software.
- Avoid using display type for ordinary copy; the Rigsolid stack should stay reserved for titles and strong banners.

### Recommended build order

1. Establish the paper background, deep-green masthead, and orange action tone.
2. Set the type hierarchy with Rigsolid for titles and Alegreya Sans for reading text.
3. Build the hero band and the promotional band as the two strongest page blocks.
4. Build the category tabs and the specimen-card grid as a reusable catalog pattern.
5. Add the detail-page split layout with a dominant illustration and compact annotation.
6. Finish the footer strip and the low-contrast legal line.

### Accessibility

- Keep the orange action color dark enough against paper and white surfaces to remain readable.
- Preserve underlines or equivalent non-color cues on links in the detail metadata.
- Keep the green title on the home hero and promo band readable against the textured background.
- Maintain visible focus states on tabs, links, and utility actions.
- Give the plate images descriptive alternative text that names the subject and the page context.
- Keep line lengths comfortable in the detail column so the reading rhythm does not collapse on smaller widths.

## Scope note

This guide covers the desktop home hero, the catalog index, the specimen detail page, and the promotional footer strip for c82.net’s Naturalist’s Library. It does not pin down mobile breakpoints, motion, loading states, or alternate article templates beyond the surfaces shown here.
